## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum image size for Amazon zoom to work?
To activate the zoom functionality on Amazon, your product image must be at least [1000 pixels on the longest side](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881). However, for the best customer experience and high-quality zoom detail, Amazon recommends using images that are [1600 pixels or larger](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881).

### Does Photoroom's free version allow for commercial use?
Photoroom's free tier includes a watermark on all images, which generally violates the [professional image standards of marketplaces like Amazon](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881). To remove watermarks and access batch processing features required for a professional relisting workflow, you must upgrade to a Pro or Max plan.

### How many images can I process at once in Pebblely?
Pebblely operates on a credit system rather than a session-based batch limit. On the Basic plan ($19/mo), you are limited to 200 images per month, while the Pro plan ($39/mo) allows for 500 images. This makes it more suitable for small, curated collections rather than massive inventory relisting.

### Why is RGB 255, 255, 255 important for Amazon?
Amazon requires a "pure white" background for all main product images to maintain a clean, consistent look across the site. If your background is even slightly off-white (e.g., RGB 250, 250, 250), Amazon's automated systems may [suppress your listing](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881), making it invisible to search results until the image is corrected.
