## Where Each Falls Short

![Where Each Falls Short](/img/shopify-magic-ai-vs-photoroom-for-sellers/inline-5.webp)


Run a "zoom-test" on your mobile device to check for pixelation before pushing new images live. Open your storefront on a high-resolution smartphone; if the product edges look jagged, your tool's resolution cap is hurting your conversion rate.

Shopify Magic's biggest limitation is its strict [1 megapixel (MP) resolution](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-magic/media-generation) cap. For context, [Amazon requires images to be at least 1000 pixels](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G181) on the longest side to enable the zoom function, though they recommend 1600 pixels. Shopify's 1MP output (often 1024x1024) barely hits the minimum requirement and can look blurry on modern retina displays or when a customer tries to inspect fabric textures. Additionally, Shopify Magic lacks batch processing, forcing sellers to edit every single product image manually in the file editor.

Photoroom’s Pro plan restricts batch processing to 50 images per session, creating a frustrating bottleneck for sellers updating large seasonal catalogs. If you have 200 new arrivals, you must run the batch tool four separate times, re-selecting settings each time. Furthermore, Photoroom's billing structure can be confusing; the mobile app often prompts for weekly subscriptions that are significantly more expensive than the annual plan. Users frequently report selecting the weekly billing option by mistake, leading to higher effective monthly costs that eat into profit margins.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Shopify Magic AI work for Amazon product images?
While you can download images from Shopify to upload to Amazon, Shopify Magic's [1-megapixel limit](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-magic/media-generation) often results in images that are too low-resolution for Amazon's zoom feature. Amazon recommends [1600 pixels on the longest side](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G181) for optimal quality, which Shopify Magic cannot consistently provide.

### Can I use Photoroom for free without watermarks?
No, the Photoroom free tier includes a watermark on all exported images. To remove watermarks and access batch editing features, you must subscribe to the Pro plan, which starts at $12.99/month.

### What is the fastest way to edit 500 product images?
Using a tool with a session cap like Photoroom would require 10 separate batch sessions. To edit 500 images efficiently, you need a dedicated bulk AI generator like PixelMatch that allows you to upload the entire catalog at once and apply consistent background and lighting settings across all files.

### Is AI image generation allowed on Etsy and TikTok Shop?
Yes, both platforms allow AI-enhanced images, but they have strict policies against misleading customers. The product itself must be an accurate representation of what the customer will receive; however, using AI to [generate backgrounds or enhance lighting](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000338367-How-to-List-an-Item-on-Etsy) is a standard practice for professional sellers.
