# Amazon Premium A+ Content vs Standard A+ Content: 2026 Comparison

As of May 2026, Amazon Premium A+ Content is free and enabled by default for all Brand Registered sellers. Premium A+ offers 7 modules per ASIN, 1464px wide images, and video, while Standard A+ is limited to 5 modules and 970px widths.

## TL;DR Verdict

![TL;DR Verdict](/img/amazon-premium-a-plus-content-vs-standard-comparison/inline-1.webp)


Audit your Brand Registry status immediately to confirm you have access to the expanded module suite that was previously locked behind strict eligibility gates. On [May 18, 2026](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G202102950), Amazon removed the historical eligibility gate—which previously required 15 approved submissions and a published Brand Story—making Premium A+ the default standard for all Brand Registered sellers.

The shift from Standard to Premium is not merely a cosmetic upgrade; it is a fundamental change in how much "digital shelf" space you control. Premium A+ allows [7 modules per ASIN](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G202102950) and [1464px wide images](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G202102950), providing a significant surface area advantage over Standard’s [5 modules](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G202102950) and [970px width](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G202102950). 

Data from Amazon's internal testing indicates that Premium A+ can drive up to a [20% conversion lift](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G202102950), whereas Standard A+ averages an [8% lift](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G202102950). Because Premium A+ is now accessible to your competitors, upgrading your listing visuals is no longer an optional "premium" strategy—it is a baseline requirement to maintain your current conversion rates.

**Actionable Step:** Log into [Seller Central](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/stories/amazon-seller-central-error-code-img-9002-fix), navigate to **Advertising > A+ Content Manager**, and start a new draft. If the "Premium" module types (like Video or Navigation Carousels) are selectable, you must begin migrating your top-selling SKUs to the 1464px format immediately.

## Side-by-Side Feature Table

![Side-by-Side Feature Table](/img/amazon-premium-a-plus-content-vs-standard-comparison/inline-2.webp)


Resize your existing high-resolution assets to 1464px before attempting to build a Premium A+ layout to avoid the "low-resolution" rejection flag. The following table breaks down the technical specifications and feature sets that distinguish the two tiers in 2026.

| Feature | Standard A+ Content | Premium A+ Content |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Maximum Modules** | [5 Modules](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G202102950) | [7 Modules](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G202102950) |
| **Image Width** | [970 pixels](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G202102950) | [1464 pixels](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G202102950) |
| **Video Integration** | No | [1920x1080px Auto-play](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G202102950) |
| **Interactive Elements** | Basic Comparison Charts | [Hotspots, Carousels, Q&A](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G202102950) |
| **Mobile Optimization** | Standard scaling | Advanced responsive stacking |
| **Eligibility** | Brand Registered | [Free for all Brand Registered](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G202102950) |

The most striking difference for a desktop user is the visual immersion. Standard A+ modules are centered on the page with significant white gutters on either side. Premium A+ modules occupy the full width of the content area, creating a "website-within-a-website" feel that prevents the user's eye from wandering to the "Products related to this item" or "Sponsored products" sections.

Standard A+ is capped at [970px](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G202102950), whereas Premium expands to a full-width [1464px](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G202102950). This extra 494 pixels of width allows for much more detailed lifestyle photography and clearer text overlays. Furthermore, Premium unlocks interactive hotspots where a customer can hover over a specific part of a product image to see a popup text box—an invaluable tool for explaining complex technical features without cluttering the main graphic.

**Actionable Step:** Export your current 970px master files. If you do not have the original high-res versions to scale up to 1464px, use an AI upscaler or PixelMatch to regenerate the backgrounds at the required width to ensure they pass the 2026 quality check.

## Best For (By Seller Profile)

![Best For (By Seller Profile)](/img/amazon-premium-a-plus-content-vs-standard-comparison/inline-4.webp)


Prioritize your ASINs for Premium upgrades based on their current traffic and revenue potential. While Premium is now free, the time investment required to build out seven high-quality modules means you should still be strategic about where you deploy it.

Standard A+ is best for new sellers or "placeholder" listings. If you have just launched a product and need to quickly replace plain text descriptions with basic [970x600px](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G202102950) image headers to establish baseline brand trust, Standard is sufficient. It requires fewer assets and is faster to set up for low-traffic SKUs that aren't yet generating enough revenue to justify a full Premium creative overhaul.

Premium A+ is best for high-traffic ASINs, premium-priced items, and complex products that benefit from [1920x1080px](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G202102950) video demonstrations. If a product has a high "return-to-page" rate, it usually means customers are looking for more information before committing. Premium modules like the Q&A or the interactive hover hotspots can provide that information and close the sale.

Premium A+ is also highly recommended for brands actively defending their listings against aggressive competitors. The wider 1464px layout and the 7-module vertical length push competitor ads—such as "Products related to this item"—much further down the page, effectively increasing your "share of eye" on the mobile and desktop screen.

**Actionable Step:** Identify your top 20% of ASINs by revenue. These are your "Hero ASINs." Schedule a Premium A+ overhaul for these SKUs this week, focusing on replacing the 970px headers with 1464px interactive carousels.

## Where Each Falls Short

![Where Each Falls Short](/img/amazon-premium-a-plus-content-vs-standard-comparison/inline-5.webp)


Run a manual quality check on a 27-inch desktop monitor to see exactly where your current Standard A+ content is failing. 

Standard A+ Shortfalls: The [970px width](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G202102950) is the most glaring weakness. On modern high-resolution monitors, a 970px image looks like a small column in the middle of a vast white screen. This breaks visual immersion and makes the brand look "small" or outdated. Furthermore, Standard A+ lacks video integration, which is a major disadvantage in 2026, where video content is the primary driver of social-commerce conversions. It restricts deep storytelling because the 5-module limit often forces sellers to choose between showing "How to Use" and "Brand Values."

Premium A+ Shortfalls: The primary drawback is the "Asset Hunger" of the format. It requires significantly more high-resolution creative assets to look professional. Uploading low-res images stretched to 1464px will result in immediate rejection under Amazon's 2026 Content Quality Analysis scoring. This automated system checks for pixelation, text-to-image ratios, and blurriness. If your assets aren't native 1464px or higher, the system will block the upload, creating a frustrating loop of rejections for sellers without a high-end design pipeline.

Managing the asset pipeline for Premium A+ across dozens of ASINs is a major operational bottleneck. This is where many sellers get stuck—they have the *permission* to use Premium A+, but they don't have the *content* to fill it.
