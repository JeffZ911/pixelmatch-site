## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does my clothing clip in TikTok Symphony?
Clothing clipping occurs because the AI is trying to wrap a 2D product image around a 3D digital human mesh. If the dimensions of your product image don't perfectly match the avatar's body proportions, or if the avatar moves in a way the software can't calculate, the "body" will appear to poke through the "fabric."

### What is the best image size for TikTok Shop in 2026?
TikTok Shop recommends a 1:1 aspect ratio with a minimum of 600 x 600 pixels. However, for the best quality on high-resolution mobile screens, many sellers use 1200 x 1200 pixels to ensure the product remains sharp when customers zoom in.

### Can I use AI-generated models on Amazon?
Yes, as long as the images meet [Amazon's technical specifications](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G1881), including a pure white background for the main image and no distracting text or watermarks. Using a tool like PixelMatch helps ensure these images look realistic enough to pass Amazon's quality checks.

### How many images can I batch-process in Photoroom?
Photoroom Pro allows you to process up to 50 images at once in their Batch Mode. This is ideal for small collections but may be slow for large-scale multi-platform apparel sellers who need to update entire catalogs.
