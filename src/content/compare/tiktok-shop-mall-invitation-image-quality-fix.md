## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why was my TikTok Shop Mall application rejected for image quality?
TikTok Shop Mall requires a "premium" shopping experience. Rejections typically happen because of low-resolution images (under 600x600 px), the use of watermarks, or "noisy" backgrounds that distract from the product. Mall curators look for studio-quality photography that builds consumer trust.

### Does TikTok Shop Mall require a white background for all images?
While not strictly mandated for every secondary image, TikTok highly recommends a plain white background for the main (cover) image. This ensures the marketplace looks clean and professional, which is a core requirement for Mall-tier status.

### Can I use AI-generated backgrounds for TikTok Shop Mall?
Yes, as long as the product itself is not distorted and the background looks realistic. Tools like PixelMatch and Photoroom generate AI backgrounds that simulate professional studio lighting, which can help your products meet the "premium" criteria without the cost of a physical photoshoot.

### What is the maximum file size for TikTok Shop images?
Each image must be under 5MB. If your AI-generated images are too large, you must compress them or use a tool that automatically optimizes the file size for web upload while maintaining the 600x600 px resolution.

### How many images should I have per listing for TikTok Shop Mall?
You should aim for at least 3-5 high-quality images. The first should be a clean product shot on a white background, followed by detail shots (close-ups of materials or features) and at least one lifestyle shot showing the product in use.
