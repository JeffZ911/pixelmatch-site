## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Shopify Magic work for Amazon images?
Shopify Magic can remove backgrounds, but it does not automatically ensure the output meets [Amazon's strict pure white background requirement](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881). You may still need to manually verify that the hex code is #FFFFFF. Additionally, Shopify Magic edits images one at a time, which is inefficient for the large catalogs typically found on Amazon.

### Does Adobe Firefly offer bulk background removal?
Adobe Express (which uses Firefly) offers a "Quick Action" for background removal, but it is primarily designed for individual images. While you can upload multiple files to a folder in Adobe Express, you generally have to trigger the background removal process on an image-by-image basis within the editor. It is not a true "hands-off" batch processor for hundreds of items.

### What is the best resolution for ecommerce images in 2026?
Most major platforms, including Shopify and Etsy, recommend a minimum of [2000 to 2048 pixels](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/product-media/image-sizes) on the shortest side. This resolution ensures that the "zoom" feature works correctly on mobile and desktop devices without the image becoming pixelated. Always aim for a 1:1 square aspect ratio for primary listing photos to maintain consistency across marketplaces.

### Can I use Shopify Magic for my Etsy store?
Not directly. Since Shopify Magic is built into the Shopify admin, you would have to upload the photo to Shopify, edit it, download the edited version, and then upload it to [Etsy](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015663347-Requirements-for-Listing-Photos). This "double-handling" of files is time-consuming and can lead to version control issues. A dedicated tool like PixelMatch is better for multi-platform distribution.
