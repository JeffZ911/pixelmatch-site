## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Amazon allow AI-generated product images in 2026?
Yes, Amazon allows AI-generated images as long as they accurately represent the product and comply with all standard [image requirements](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881). The main image must still be a professional photograph (or a highly realistic render) of the actual product on a pure white background, without any added promotional text or watermarks.

### Can Midjourney v7 generate the 255, 255, 255 white background required by Amazon?
It can, but it is inconsistent. Even with the `--no shadows, reflections` parameters, Midjourney often adds subtle gradients or "off-white" tints to make the image look more artistic. To ensure a [pure white background](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881), you usually have to use a secondary tool like Removebg or Adobe Express, or use a dedicated tool like PixelMatch that handles this natively.

### How many pixels should my Amazon images be for the zoom feature?
Amazon requires a minimum of 1000 pixels on the longest side, but for the best customer experience and to enable the "Zoom" function properly, [1600 pixels or larger](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881) is recommended. PixelMatch generates images at these high resolutions by default, whereas Midjourney may require an additional "Upscale" step.

### What is the biggest risk of using Midjourney for product photos?
The biggest risk is "Product Drift." This occurs when the AI subtly changes the proportions, colors, or textures of your product. If a customer receives a product that feels "cheaper" or "different" than the AI-enhanced photo, they are likely to leave a [1-star review](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G200386250), which is significantly more expensive to fix than the cost of a proper photography tool.
