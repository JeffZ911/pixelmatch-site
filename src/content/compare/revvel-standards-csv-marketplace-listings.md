## Where Each Falls Short

![Where Each Falls Short](/img/revvel-standards-csv-marketplace-listings/inline-5.webp)


Check for special characters—like smart quotes (“ ”) or hidden line breaks—in your "description" field before uploading; these are the most common reasons a CSV batch fails the Reverb validation check.

### Manual CSV Failures
The manual approach is highly prone to syntax errors. Reverb's importer is sensitive; a single missing column or an incorrect text string in the "condition" field will cause the entire CSV to fail. For example, you must use exact text strings like "Mint" or "Very Good" rather than numerical IDs. Furthermore, relying on Google Drive or Dropbox for image URLs often fails because these platforms frequently block "scraping" or direct-link requests from marketplace servers.

### Automated Tool Limitations
Monthly recurring costs can eat into margins, especially for low-margin items. Additionally, API-based tools are subject to "rate limits." If you try to push 500 listings to a marketplace in five minutes, the platform's API may temporarily throttle your account, leading to delays that a CSV upload would bypass.

### Image Hosting Workarounds
Many sellers try to use free image hosting sites to populate their CSVs, but these links often expire or break after a few months. When a link breaks, your listing loses its photos, which directly leads to a drop in conversion rates and potential delisting by the platform for policy violations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the required headers for a Reverb CSV?
The essential headers for a Reverb CSV include `sku`, `make`, `model`, `title`, `description`, `price`, and `condition_id`. While the header is named `condition_id`, the platform actually requires the text name of the condition, such as "Mint" or "Excellent," as specified in the Reverb Bulk Upload Guide.

### Why do my Google Drive image links fail in my CSV?
Google Drive links are not direct image paths; they lead to a viewer page that requires browser execution. Reverb’s CSV importer requires a direct link to the image file (ending in .jpg or .png). To fix this, you must use a dedicated image hosting service or a tool like PixelMatch that provides static, publicly accessible URLs for every generated image.

### Is there a limit to how many items I can upload via CSV?
Reverb does not explicitly cap the number of items in a single CSV, but larger files (over 1,000 rows) are more likely to time out or encounter processing errors. For the best results, break your inventory into batches of 500 SKUs to ensure the platform can process the images and data without interruption.

### Does Reverb charge extra for using automated listing tools?
No, Reverb does not charge an additional fee for using third-party API tools like Vendoo or List Perfectly. You will still pay the standard 5% selling fee and payment processing costs. The only extra cost is the subscription fee charged by the tool provider itself.
