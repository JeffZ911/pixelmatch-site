## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens if my Amazon listing is suppressed during Prime Day?
If your listing is "Search Suppressed," it will not appear in customer search results or browse categories. While the listing still technically exists, customers can only find it if they have the direct URL. This effectively drops your sales to zero and can cause your organic ranking to plummet for weeks after the event ends.

### Why does Amazon require exactly RGB 255, 255, 255?
Amazon uses the pure white background requirement to create a consistent, clean shopping experience across all devices. "Off-white" or greyish backgrounds (like RGB 250, 250, 250) can look dirty on mobile screens and interfere with Amazon’s automated image-recognition software, which is why they mandate a [pure white background](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/1881).

### Can I use lifestyle images as my Amazon main image?
No. Amazon’s policy for the "Main" image slot is strictly a product-only shot on a pure white background. You should use lifestyle images (showing the product in use or in a setting) for your 2nd through 7th image slots and your A+ Content, but using one as the main image will result in immediate listing suppression.

### Is Photoroom's batch limit really 50 images?
Yes, on the standard Pro plan, Photoroom limits batch edits to 50 images at a time. This is designed for small business owners and creators. For enterprise-level batching of hundreds or thousands of images, a dedicated ecommerce tool like PixelMatch is more efficient.
