## Pricing Comparison

![Pricing Comparison](/img/google-imagen-3-vs-midjourney-v7-for-products/inline-3.webp)


The "cost per image" is a deceptive metric. A $0.02 image that takes 10 prompts to get right is more expensive than a $0.50 image generated perfectly on the first try by a specialized tool. However, understanding the base overhead of these foundation models is essential for your P&L.

### Midjourney v7 Subscription Tiers
Midjourney uses a traditional SaaS subscription model. You pay for "Fast Hours" (GPU time). Once those run out, you either buy more or drop to "Relax Mode" (on higher tiers).

*   **Basic Plan ($10/mo):** Roughly 200 generations per month. No Relax mode. Best for hobbyists or very small stores.
*   **Standard Plan ($30/mo):** 15 hours of Fast generation and unlimited Relax mode. This is the "sweet spot" for most independent sellers.
*   **Pro Plan ($60/mo):** 30 hours of Fast generation and Stealth Mode (hides your images from the public gallery).

### Google Imagen 3 API Pricing
Google charges per successful request through Vertex AI. This is a "pay-as-you-go" model, which is highly scalable but requires a developer setup.

*   **Imagen 3 Fast: $0.02 per image:** Optimized for speed and lower cost.
*   **Imagen 3 High Quality: $0.03 per image:** Best for final production assets.
*   **Operational Costs:** Unlike Midjourney, Google Cloud requires setting up a billing project, API keys, and potentially a middleware to handle the requests.

**Actionable Step:** Calculate your monthly image needs. If you need 5,000 images for a seasonal catalog update, Midjourney’s $30 "Unlimited" Relax mode is the most cost-effective. If you are building an automated backend for a custom Shopify app, the $0.02/image API from Google is the only logical choice.

## Where Each Falls Short

![Where Each Falls Short](/img/google-imagen-3-vs-midjourney-v7-for-products/inline-5.webp)


Neither Google nor Midjourney was built specifically for ecommerce. They are general-purpose creative engines, which leads to several "last mile" problems for sellers.

### The Accuracy Gap
Midjourney v7 still struggles with exact deterministic edits. If you have a specific logo with a unique font, Midjourney will likely "hallucinate" or warp it slightly. For a professional seller, a warped logo is an instant "Return to Sender" or a rejected Amazon listing.

### The Technical Hurdle
Google Imagen 3's best features are locked behind the Google Cloud Platform (GCP). For the average Shopify seller, navigating IAM roles, API quotas, and JSON payloads is a massive hurdle. Without a developer, you are stuck using the basic Gemini interface, which lacks the granular control needed for professional product photography.

### The Compliance Crisis
Neither tool automatically resizes images to [Amazon's recommended 2000x2000 px zoom specs](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881). Furthermore, getting a "true" RGB 255,255,255 white background in Midjourney is notoriously difficult—it often adds "artistic" shadows or off-white tints that lead to listing suppressions.

**Actionable Step:** Always run your generated images through a "Compliance Check." Open the image in a basic editor and use the eyedropper tool on the background. If the RGB value isn't exactly 255, 255, 255, do not upload it as your Amazon Main Image.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use Midjourney or Imagen images as my Amazon Main Image?
Only if they meet the strict [Amazon Image Standards](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881). This includes a pure white background (RGB 255, 255, 255), no watermarks, and the product occupying at least 85% of the frame. Midjourney often fails the "pure white" test without significant post-processing or background removal.

### Which AI tool is better for generating text on product labels?
Google Imagen 3 is significantly better at text rendering than Midjourney v7. While Midjourney has improved, it still frequently makes spelling errors or uses "gibberish" characters on smaller label details. Imagen 3 was specifically trained to handle dense text, making it the better choice for packaging mockups.

### Do I own the copyright to images generated by Midjourney or Google?
The legal landscape for AI copyright is evolving. According to Midjourney's Terms of Service, you own the assets you create if you are a paid member. However, the US Copyright Office has generally ruled that AI-generated images without significant human input cannot be copyrighted. Always consult a legal professional regarding your specific brand assets.

### How do I get a 2000x2000px image from these tools?
Midjourney typically outputs 1024x1024px images. You must use their internal "Upscale" buttons (Subtle or Creative) to reach higher resolutions. Google Imagen 3 allows for higher resolution requests via the API, but for most ecommerce platforms, you will still need a dedicated AI upscaler to reach the high-fidelity [2000px+ requirements for Etsy zoom](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015663347-Requirements-and-Best-Practices-for-Images-in-Your-Etsy-Shop).
