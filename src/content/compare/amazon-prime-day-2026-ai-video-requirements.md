## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the maximum file size for Amazon product videos in 2026?
Amazon currently caps video file sizes at <a href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=500MB&tag=jeffzen911-20" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" target="_blank">500MB</a>. If your AI-generated video from a tool like Runway exceeds this, you must use a compressor or lower the bitrate to ensure it uploads correctly to Seller Central.

### Can I use AI-generated music in my Amazon Prime Day videos?
Yes, but you must ensure you have the full commercial rights to the audio. Tools like Canva Pro provide [royalty-free music tracks](https://www.canva.com/help/using-content-for-commercial-purposes/) that are safe for Amazon use, whereas using a popular copyrighted song via an AI "cover" tool will result in an immediate ad suspension.

### Does Amazon require a disclosure for AI-generated product videos?
As of 2026, Amazon's policies focus on [content authenticity](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G200386250). While they do not always require a "[Made with AI](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/stories/meta-made-with-ai-label-removal-hack)" watermark for [lifestyle backgrounds](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/stories/krea-2-turbo-ecommerce-lifestyle-backgrounds), you must not use AI to alter the physical appearance or functionality of the actual product, as this is considered "materially different" and can lead to account suspension for "Inauthentic" complaints.

### What is the best video length for Sponsored Brands ads during Prime Day?
Amazon allows videos between 6 and 45 seconds, but <a href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=15+to+30+seconds&tag=jeffzen911-20" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" target="_blank">15 to 30 seconds</a> is the "sweet spot" for conversion. During Prime Day, shoppers are moving fast; if your [AI video](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/stories/tiktok-shop-symphony-ai-video-to-listing-workflow) doesn't show the product in the first 3 seconds, they will scroll past.
