### Canva Magic Media: The Credit-Based Model
Canva’s pricing is more complex because it uses a credit system shared across its "Magic Studio" suite.
*   **Free Tier:** Highly restricted. Users typically receive only [5 lifetime video generation credits](https://www.canva.com/help/magic-media/) for Magic Media.
*   **Canva Pro:** Costs $119.99 per year (or $14.99 monthly). Pro users receive [500 monthly credits](https://www.canva.com/help/magic-media/) for AI generations, which include both image and video creation.
*   **Canva for Teams:** Costs $100 per year per person (minimum 3 people). This tier is better for larger ecommerce agencies but follows similar credit limits per user.

### Amazon-Only FBA Sellers
If 90% of your revenue comes from FBA, Amazon’s native tool is the clear winner. It is the fastest way to test "Sponsored Brands video hooks" without a production budget. Because it pulls data directly from your Amazon Creative Assets, it ensures that your product title, price, and Prime badge are displayed correctly. It is specifically designed to bypass the common rejection reasons that plague third-party videos.

### Multi-Channel Brands (Shopify, TikTok, Etsy)
Brands that need to adapt content across Shopify, TikTok Shop, and Instagram Reels should prioritize Canva Pro. Canva allows you to create a single 4-second AI clip and instantly "Magic Switch" it into multiple dimensions. While Amazon's tool is getting better at vertical video (9:16), it is still fundamentally optimized for the Amazon storefront experience.

### High-Volume Catalog Managers
Sellers with hundreds of SKUs will find both video tools slow if handled manually. The most efficient workflow for a large catalog is to focus on batch-generating high-converting static images first. Once you have a library of 50+ professional images per SKU, you can feed those into Amazon's generator to create a "slideshow" style video that highlights different angles of the product in motion.

## Where Each Falls Short

![Where Each Falls Short](/img/amazon-ai-video-generator-vs-canva-magic/inline-5.webp)


Check for "AI hallucinations," such as floating objects, blurred logos, or distorted product edges, in the first 2 seconds of every generated clip. AI motion models are still prone to "melting" products if the original image is not clear.

### Amazon Video Generator: The "Template" Trap
The primary drawback of <a href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Amazon%27s+native+tool&tag=jeffzen911-20" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Amazon's native tool</a> is its rigidity. You are largely confined to specific templates and motion styles. You cannot "prompt" the AI to make your product fly through a ring of fire or submerge in water—you are limited to the motion presets Amazon deems "shoppable." Furthermore, while you can download videos from Creative Assets, they are clearly branded with Amazon-style overlays which may look out of place on a high-end Shopify store.

### Canva Magic Media: The 4-Second Limit
Canva’s AI video generation is currently limited to [4-second clips](https://www.canva.com/help/magic-media/). While you can stitch multiple clips together, creating a cohesive 30-second product demo requires significant manual editing. Additionally, Canva lacks direct ecommerce integration. It won't know if your price changed or if your item went out of stock. If you create a video in Canva, you must manually ensure it meets <a href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Amazon%E2%80%99s+500MB+file+size+limit&tag=jeffzen911-20" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Amazon’s 500MB file size limit</a> and specific bitrate requirements.

### The Source Image Problem
Both generators animate existing assets rather than creating perfect product shots from scratch. If your product is obscured or the lighting is poor, the AI will often "hallucinate" details to fill the gaps, resulting in a video that looks "uncanny" or fake. This is why professional sellers use PixelMatch to create the high-resolution "anchor images" that give the AI a solid foundation to work from.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use the videos I make in Amazon Seller Central on TikTok?
Yes, you can download your generated videos from the Creative Assets library in [Seller Central](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/stories/amazon-seller-central-error-code-img-9002-fix). However, be aware that these videos often include Amazon-specific layouts or fonts that may not align with the native "lo-fi" aesthetic of TikTok or Instagram Reels.

### How many videos can I make per month with Canva's free version?
Canva's free tier is extremely limited for AI video, usually offering only [5 lifetime credits](https://www.canva.com/help/magic-media/) for Magic Media video generation. If you plan to use AI video for an active ecommerce store, you will almost certainly need the Canva Pro plan which provides 500 monthly credits.

### Does Amazon's AI Video Generator work for all product categories?
No, Amazon restricts certain categories from using automated creative tools, particularly those in "sensitive" niches like healthcare or adult products. Most standard consumer categories (Electronics, Home, Beauty, Apparel) are fully supported, provided you have <a href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Brand+Registry&tag=jeffzen911-20" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Brand Registry</a>.

### What is the maximum resolution for Amazon Sponsored Brands videos?
Amazon recommends a resolution of <a href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=1920+x+1080+pixels&tag=jeffzen911-20" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" target="_blank">1920 x 1080 pixels</a> for 16:9 videos and 1080 x 1920 pixels for 9:16 videos. Files must be under 500MB and in .MP4 or .MOV format. Amazon’s AI tool automatically outputs files within these exact specifications.
