# Amazon Prime Day 2026 Main Image Bundle Policy: Complete Seller Guide

For Amazon Prime Day 2026, your bundle's main image [must include the specific products in the bundle](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/200442350) and exclude any props. Images must be at least [1000×1000 pixels](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/1881) on a [pure white background](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/1881).

## Detailed Requirements

![Detailed Requirements](/img/amazon-prime-day-2026-main-image-bundle-policy/inline-2.webp)


The amazon prime [day 2026 main](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/policy/amazon-prime-day-2026-main-image-compliance) image bundle policy exists to ensure customers understand exactly what they are purchasing. In a bundle, the "product" is the entire collection of items sold under a single ASIN. If your main image shows three items but the customer only receives two, you face an immediate "Product Not as Described" claim, which is lethal to your seller health during high-velocity events.

### Image Size and Formatting
While the technical minimum to avoid a hard upload error is [500 pixels](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/1881), aiming for the bare minimum is a strategic mistake for Prime Day. Amazon's systems favor listings that provide a high-quality customer experience. To enable the hover-to-zoom feature, which significantly increases conversion rates on mobile and desktop, your images must be at least [1000 pixels on the longest side](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/1881). For the sharpest display on high-resolution Retina screens, Amazon recommends [1600 pixels](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/1881).

Batch-process your bundle images to ensure every file is exported as a JPEG with a [pure white background (RGB 255, 255, 255)](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/1881). Using PixelMatch allows you to maintain this consistency across hundreds of SKUs without manually adjusting the canvas size for each individual bundle configuration.

### Bundle-Specific Content Rules
The most frequent violation of the amazon prime day 2026 main image bundle policy involves the inclusion of "lifestyle props." If you are selling a bundle of organic coffee beans and a ceramic mug, the main image must show only the beans (or their packaging) and the mug. You cannot include a spoon, a napkin, or a plate of cookies to "set the scene." 

Amazon's [Product Bundling Policy](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/200442350) is explicit: the main image must show the specific products in the bundle and nothing else. This rule extends to text and graphics. You are prohibited from adding:
*   Borders or frames.
*   Watermarks or seller logos (unless they are part of the physical product packaging).
*   Promotional text like "Limited Time Offer" or "Prime Day Exclusive."
*   In-set images or "ghost" products that aren't in the box.

### Consumables and Restricted Categories
If you are a multi-platform seller dealing in consumables (grocery, beauty, health), pay close attention to the [Product Bundling Policy](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/200442350) regarding manufacturer packaging. For many categories, bundles must consist of items that are packaged together by the original manufacturer. If you are creating "custom bundles" of different brands, ensure your main image reflects the actual secondary packaging (the box or shrink-wrap) the customer will receive. 

**Actionable Step:** Verify that your bundle's main image shows the products out of their shipping box but in their retail packaging. If the products are loose, they should be arranged neatly so that the [product occupies 85% or more of the frame](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/1881).

Listing suppression often happens at the worst possible time—usually 48 hours before Prime Day starts when traffic begins to spike. Understanding why the amazon prime day 2026 main image bundle policy triggers a rejection can help you proactively audit your catalog.

### Including Props or Excluded Items
Sellers often try to show the product "in use" to drive clicks. For a bundle of camera lenses, you might be tempted to show the lenses attached to a camera body. However, unless the camera body is part of that specific ASIN bundle, its inclusion in the main image violates the [Product Image Requirements](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/1881). This results in a "Main Image contains prohibited items" error in Seller Central. 

### Incorrect Background Color
A common mistake is using an "off-white" or light grey background. While it may look white to the human eye, Amazon’s algorithms check for the specific hex code #FFFFFF or [RGB 255, 255, 255](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/1881). If your background is 254, 255, 255, the image is technically non-compliant. This is why many sellers move away from traditional photography for bundles and instead use an ai product image batch generator to ensure a digitally perfect white background across the entire bundle catalog.

### Promotional Text and Badges
With Prime Day 2026 approaching, the urge to add "Prime Day" or "Sale" badges directly onto your image is high. Do not do this. Amazon automatically applies the "Prime Day Deal" badge to your listing if you have an active, approved deal. Manually adding text or badges to your main image is a violation that leads to [immediate listing suppression](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/1881). 

**Actionable Step:** Review your "Suppressed Listings" view in the "Fix Your Products" section of Seller Central. If a bundle is suppressed, check the "Attribute Name" column; if it says "Main Image," the background or the inclusion of text is likely the culprit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I show the packaging of the bundle in the main image?
Yes, you are encouraged to show the retail packaging in the main image, especially for bundles. However, the [product itself must be visible](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/1881), and the packaging should not obscure the items. The goal is to show the customer exactly what will arrive at their door.

### What happens if my bundle image has a slight shadow?
Amazon allows for "natural shadows" to prevent the product from looking like it is floating unnaturally. However, the shadow must not interfere with the [pure white background (RGB 255, 255, 255)](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/1881) requirement or extend outside the image frame. Excessive or "drop shadows" added in post-production are often flagged for rejection.

### Does the 85% rule apply to the entire bundle or just the main item?
The [85% coverage rule](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/1881) applies to the entire "unit" being sold. In a bundle, this means the collective group of products must fill at least 85% of the frame. If you have a large bundle of small items, arrange them so they fill the square canvas efficiently without leaving excessive white space on the sides.

### Can I use a 3D render for my bundle main image for Prime Day?
Amazon accepts high-quality 3D renders as long as they are [photorealistic and accurately represent the product](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/1881). If the render looks "cartoonish" or fails to show the textures of the actual product, it may be rejected during a manual review. For Prime Day 2026, it is safer to use high-quality photos processed through an AI tool to ensure realism.
