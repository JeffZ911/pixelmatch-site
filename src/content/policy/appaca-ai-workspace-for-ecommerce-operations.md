## Detailed Requirements

![Detailed Requirements](/img/appaca-ai-workspace-for-ecommerce-operations/inline-2.webp)


Audit your current product catalog against these platform-specific granular rules to prevent "Shadow Suppressions" where your listing remains active but is buried in search results due to poor image quality scores.

### Amazon FBA Image Rules
Amazon remains the strictest platform for visual assets. Your primary (HERO) image must be a professional photograph of the actual product being sold. Off-white backgrounds, even slightly grey (RGB 254, 254, 254), can result in automated listing removal. The product must occupy at least [85% of the image frame](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/1881). Furthermore, Amazon prohibits the use of any text, logos, watermarks, or "inset images" (small pictures within the main picture) on the primary photo. While competitors like Photoroom offer Pro tiers at $12.99/mo to help with background removal, PixelMatch is specifically engineered to handle these Amazon-specific "85% fill" rules in massive batches, ensuring your AI-generated white backgrounds are mathematically pure RGB 255.

### Shopify & Etsy Creative Freedom
Unlike Amazon, Shopify and Etsy prioritize brand storytelling. Shopify supports a wide range of formats including [JPEG, PNG, WEBP, and even HEIC](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-admin/productivity-tools/file-uploads). On Shopify, you can upload up to [250 images, 3D models, or videos](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/product-media/add-media) per SKU, allowing for deep lifestyle immersion. Etsy specifically recommends using a [2000px square image](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000338067-How-to-Protect-Listing-Images) for the best "zoom" experience and now allows sellers to upload up to [20 photos per listing](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000338067-How-to-Protect-Listing-Images). This expansion means you need more content than ever; using an **appaca ai workspace for ecommerce operations** helps you track which of those 20 slots are filled with high-performing lifestyle shots versus basic studio shots.

### TikTok Shop Authenticity Rules
TikTok Shop is highly sensitive to "misleading content." If your product image shows a specific accessory or a variation that is not included in the SKU, TikTok will flag the listing for a Product Listing Violation. They require high-resolution images where the product is clearly visible against a simple background. Because TikTok is a mobile-first platform, images should ideally be optimized for vertical viewing or square formats (1:1 aspect ratio). Authenticity is the metric here; overly "plastic" or fake-looking AI generations will be flagged by their Video Pre-Check Tool. PixelMatch solves this by using your actual product photos as the seed, ensuring the physical geometry of the item remains 100% accurate while only the environment is AI-generated.

Scan your rejected listings for these four patterns to identify why your "Active" listings might suddenly be "Suppressed" or "Inactive."

1.  **The "Dirty White" Background:** On Amazon, if your background is RGB 250, 250, 250, it looks white to the human eye but is "dirty" to the Amazon bot. This is a primary cause for image-related suppressions.
2.  **The 85% Fill Rule Violation:** Amazon requires the product to fill most of the frame. If you have too much "dead space" around a small product, the listing will be flagged. Conversely, if the product is "cropped" (edges touching the border), it will also be rejected.
3.  **File Size Bloat on Shopify:** While Shopify allows up to [20 MB files](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-admin/productivity-tools/file-uploads), uploading 100 images at that size will lead to significant page load lag. Slow page speeds increase bounce rates, which indirectly costs you money through wasted ad spend and Stripe's 2.9% + $0.30 processing fees on orders that never happen because the customer left.
4.  **Inconsistent Variations on TikTok:** If you sell a "Blue" widget but the primary image shows a "Navy" widget, TikTok's Listing Review will likely fail. This is especially common when using generic AI tools that hallucinate colors; PixelMatch prevents this by locking the product's original colors during the background generation process.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum image size for Amazon zoom?
To enable the "Zoom" feature on Amazon detail pages, your images must be at least [1000 pixels on the longest side](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/1881). However, Amazon recommends [1600 pixels or larger](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/1881) for the best customer experience. Images smaller than 500 pixels will generally be rejected entirely by the system.

### Can I use AI-generated images on Etsy?
Yes, Etsy allows AI-generated images as long as they accurately represent the product you are selling. Etsy’s primary concern is that the [main image is not blurry or dark](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000338067-How-to-Protect-Listing-Images) and that it doesn't use watermarks. Using an **appaca ai workspace for ecommerce operations** can help you keep track of which images are AI-enhanced versus original photography for transparency.

### Why did TikTok Shop reject my product listing?
The most common reason for rejection on TikTok Shop is a mismatch between the promotional images and the actual product description. TikTok requires that images match the physical product exactly in terms of color, size, and quantity. If your image shows a pack of three but you are only selling one, the listing will be flagged for a policy violation.

### How many images should I have per listing?
While requirements vary, Amazon allows up to 9 images (though only 7 usually show on the main page), and Etsy now allows up to [20 photos](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000338067-How-to-Protect-Listing-Images). Shopify supports up to [250 pieces of media](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/product-media/add-media). Generally, a "complete" listing should have at least 5-7 images: 1 HERO shot, 3 lifestyle shots, and 2-3 infographic/dimension shots.
