# TikTok Shop Image Quality Standards for 2025: Complete Seller Guide

To meet TikTok Shop image quality standards for 2025, sellers must upload up to 9 square (1:1) images with a minimum resolution of 600x600 pixels. The main image must feature a pure white background with no watermarks or promotional text.

| **Minimum Resolution** | 600 x 600 pixels | Official Seller Center Policy |

| **Image Quantity** | 1 to 9 images | Maximize "Good" Tier Status |
| **Main Image Background** | Pure White (#FFFFFF) | Mandatory for Primary Image |

## Detailed Requirements

![Detailed Requirements](/img/tiktok-shop-image-quality-standards-2025/inline-2.webp)


Configure your export settings in your photo editor to match these precise specifications. TikTok Shop’s algorithm prioritizes listings that meet the "Good" quality tier, which is heavily influenced by your [image metadata](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/policy/google-merchant-center-ai-image-metadata-requirements-2026-20260619) and visual clarity. If you are migrating listings from Amazon or Shopify, do not assume your existing assets will pass; TikTok's background-detection AI is significantly more aggressive than other platforms.

### Main Image vs. Additional Images

Your primary image (the first slot in your listing) acts as the gatekeeper for your store's health. TikTok requires a pure white background (#FFFFFF) for this specific slot. Off-white, light gray, or even slightly textured backgrounds will trigger an automated "Image Quality Issue" notification. This image must show the product clearly without any watermarks, borders, placeholders, or promotional text. Avoid adding "Best Seller" badges or "Free Shipping" icons directly onto the image; TikTok provides dynamic overlays for these features that do not violate policy.

Additional images (slots 2 through 9) allow for more creative freedom. Use these slots for lifestyle shots, infographics, and size charts. While the background rule is relaxed for these slots, the 1:1 aspect ratio remains mandatory. If you upload a 4:5 portrait image, TikTok will automatically crop it, potentially cutting off vital product information or your brand logo.

### Understanding Listing Quality Tiers

TikTok categorizes every product listing into "Poor," "Fair," or "Good" tiers. To achieve the "Good" tier—which is essential for appearing in the Shop Tab's organic recommendations—you must upload at least 5 high-quality images. These images should cover:
1.  The product from the front (Main Image).
2.  The product from the back or side.
3.  A close-up of the material or key feature.
4.  The product in use (lifestyle).
5.  Packaging or what's included in the box.

Maintaining a high listing [quality score](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/policy/etsy-search-quality-score-image-requirements-2026) directly impacts your Product Search Ranking within the app. Listings with only one or two images are often flagged as "incomplete" and receive significantly less traffic from the algorithm.

Identify the root cause of your listing rejections by checking the "Product Diagnostic" tool in the TikTok Shop Seller Center. Most rejections are not caused by human reviewers but by automated vision models that scan for specific "illegal" pixels.

### Non-Compliant Backgrounds

The most frequent rejection reason is failing the automated background check. TikTok's AI looks for a specific color value of #FFFFFF. If your photographer used a physical white backdrop that has shadows in the corners, the AI will register these as gray gradients and reject the image. Even high-end tools like Photoroom's Pro tier at $12.99/mo can occasionally leave a 1-pixel border of the original background, which is enough to trigger a flag. Ensure your background removal tool is set to "Hard Edge" or "Pure White" to avoid this loop.

### Low Resolution and Compression Artifacts

Images falling below the 600x600 pixel threshold are blocked at the upload stage. However, a common pitfall is uploading an 800x800 image that has been heavily compressed. TikTok applies its own compression upon upload to ensure fast loading on mobile devices. If your original file is already pixelated, the secondary compression will make the product look "untrustworthy" to the AI, leading to a "Low Quality Image" rejection. Aim for a file size close to the 5MB limit to ensure enough data is present for a clean render.

### Prohibited Overlays and Brand Infringement

Including brand logos (other than your own), promotional text, or watermarks on the main image violates the Product [Listing Policy](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/policy/tiktok-shop-listing-policy-crackdown-2026-20260616). This includes subtle watermarks often added by stock photo sites or supplier catalogs. TikTok also scans for "clickbait" elements, such as fake "Play" buttons or notification badges drawn onto the image. If you are selling a multi-pack, the main image must show the exact number of items the customer will receive; using a "3-pack" text overlay instead of showing three items visually is a common reason for rejection.

## Optimizing for the 2025 Mobile Experience

TikTok is a mobile-first platform, and its 2025 updates place a heavy emphasis on how images look on high-refresh-rate displays. The "Good" listing tier is not just a suggestion; it is a requirement for sellers who want to participate in TikTok Shop "Deals" events or "Flash Sales."

### Mobile-First Cropping

Because the TikTok Shop interface is dense with information (price, vouchers, ratings, and "Sold" counts), your product needs to occupy at least 80% of the image frame. If your product is too small within the 1:1 square, it will be unclickable on a mobile screen. When using PixelMatch, use the "Auto-Zoom" feature to ensure the product is centered and fills the frame appropriately for a 6.7-inch smartphone screen.

### The Rise of WEBP and HEIC

As of early 2025, TikTok has expanded its [API support for WEBP and HEIC](https://partner.tiktokshop.com/doc/page/271813). These formats provide superior quality at smaller file sizes compared to traditional JPGs. If you are shooting on an iPhone, you no longer need to convert HEIC files to JPG before uploading, which preserves more color depth and detail. However, ensure your batch processing tool supports these formats to avoid "Unsupported File Type" errors during bulk uploads.
