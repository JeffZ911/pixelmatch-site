## Etsy AI Model Disclosure Badge Guide: Navigating the 2026 Rules

![Etsy AI Model Disclosure Badge Guide: Navigating the 2026 Rules](/img/etsy-ai-model-disclosure-badge-guide/inline-1.webp)


**Quick Answer:** As of June 9, 2026, sellers using AI human models in mockups must add a conspicuous [disclosure badge](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/stories/tiktok-shop-ai-content-disclosure-badge-guide) to comply with New York's Synthetic Performer Law. Etsy also requires ticking the 'I used AI-generative technology' box in the listing editor to remain compliant with their [Creativity Standards](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/stories/etsy-ai-image-disclosure-2026-case-study).

Navigate the intersection of platform-level rules and state-level laws by updating your listing workflow. Etsy’s internal policies focus on buyer transparency regarding the "maker," while the New York Synthetic Performer Law focuses on the "performer" (the model) depicted in the media.

### Etsy's Creativity Standards for AI
Etsy updated its [Creativity Standards](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/stories/etsy-ai-image-disclosure-2026-case-study) to ensure buyers know when AI played a role in the creation of an item. When you create or edit a listing, you must tick the "I used AI-generative technology" checkbox. This is located in the "About this listing" section of the editor.

Furthermore, you must adjust your attribution settings. If the physical product itself is generated by AI (such as a 3D-printed item where the mesh was AI-generated), you must categorize it as "Designed by a seller" rather than "Made by a seller". This distinction is vital; Etsy's search algorithm and Trust & Safety team use these data points to verify that you are maintaining the "Human Touch" required by the platform. If you use AI to create a digital download, like a wall art printable, the same "Designed by" rule applies.

### The NY Synthetic Performer Law
The N.Y. Gen. Bus. Law § 396-b introduces a much stricter requirement for visual assets. If your listing image features a "synthetic performer"—a digitally created human that looks real—you must include a "clear and conspicuous" disclosure. 

This disclosure must be a text overlay or badge directly on the image. Phrases like "[AI Model](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/policy/tiktok-shop-ai-model-policy-leak-2026) Used," "Synthetic Performer," or "Digitally Generated Model" are acceptable. The law requires the text to be easily readable against the background. Using a tool like Adobe Express or Canva to add a high-contrast label in the corner of your image is the standard practice for compliance.

Note that AI-generated backgrounds (like those created in PixelMatch) do not trigger the synthetic performer law. If you place a real product or a real human model in an AI-generated kitchen or forest, no "Synthetic Performer" badge is required. This makes AI backgrounds a lower-risk visual strategy for sellers who want to avoid cluttering their [lifestyle mockup](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015663347-Requirements-for-Listing-Photos) with legal disclaimers.

## Avoid These Three Common Policy Rejection Triggers

![Avoid These Three Common Policy Rejection Triggers](/img/etsy-ai-model-disclosure-badge-guide/inline-4.webp)


Identify the gaps in your current listing process before Etsy’s automated systems or buyer reports flag your shop. Compliance is not just about the image; it is about the metadata and description consistency.

1.  **Missing AI Disclosure in Descriptions:** Even if you check the AI box in the backend, failing to state AI involvement in the listing description can lead to buyer reports. If a buyer feels "tricked" by a hyper-realistic [AI model](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/policy/tiktok-shop-ai-model-policy-leak-2026), they can report the listing for "Not as Described." Etsy’s trust and safety teams prioritize listings with clear, factual descriptions. A simple sentence like "Lifestyle images feature AI-generated models to show fit and scale" prevents these disputes.
2.  **Omission of the $1,000-Penalty Badge:** Using an AI-generated human model from a tool like PhotoAI or Booth.AI without a clear [disclosure badge](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/stories/tiktok-shop-ai-content-disclosure-badge-guide) is now a direct violation of the N.Y. S8420A transparency mandate. In 2026, regulatory bodies use automated visual scrapers to identify non-compliant commercial images. A single unbadged mockup could result in a fine that exceeds the annual profit of a small Etsy shop.
3.  **Incorrect Attribution for Digital Art:** Many sellers incorrectly select "Made by a seller" for AI-generated digital artwork. Etsy's [Creativity Standards](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/stories/etsy-ai-image-disclosure-2026-case-study) are explicit: if the primary creative output was generated by a prompt, you are the designer, not the maker. Selecting "Made by" for a 100% AI-generated image is considered a platform violation and can lead to permanent shop suspension.

## Execute the Compliance Fix: Badging and Background Strategies

![Execute the Compliance Fix: Badging and Background Strategies](/img/etsy-ai-model-disclosure-badge-guide/inline-5.webp)


Apply these three fixes to your shop today to insulate your business from legal risk and platform penalties.

### Add a "Synthetic Performer" Badge
Add a highly visible "[AI Model](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/policy/tiktok-shop-ai-model-policy-leak-2026) Used" or "Synthetic Performer" badge to the bottom-right or top-left corner of any listing image that features an AI-generated person. To meet the "conspicuous" requirement of the NY law, ensure the text is at least 12pt font and has a high-contrast background (e.g., white text on a black semi-transparent box). 

If you are using [2000×2000 px minimum](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015663347-Requirements-for-Listing-Photos) images as recommended by Etsy, your badge should be at least 300px wide to remain legible on mobile devices.

### Update Listing Descriptions in Bulk
Update all existing listing descriptions to include a factual, one-sentence disclosure about AI usage near the top of the text. You can use Etsy's "Bulk Edit" tool to add this to the "Description" field of all listings in a specific section. 

*Example Text:* "This listing utilizes AI-generated lifestyle mockups to demonstrate product use. The physical item is handmade/designed by [Your Shop Name]."
