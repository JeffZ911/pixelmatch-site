### Image and Listing Rules

Your main product image is the first point of contact for a customer and the first point of failure for a compliance check. TikTok Shop requires a clean, professional look that focuses entirely on the product. Unlike Amazon, which allows a [2000x2000 px recommendation](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G1881) for high-zoom capabilities, TikTok prioritizes fast loading and mobile-first aspect ratios.

1.  **Visual Clarity:** Main images must show the product clearly against a professional background. You must exclude watermarks, borders, and promotional text. If you are selling a bundle, every item in the bundle must be visible in the primary image.
2.  **Title Precision:** Your product titles are capped at 255 characters. Avoid "clickbait" terms. Using words like "Discount," "Cheap," or "Best Seller" in the title is a direct violation of the Listing Guidelines and will result in the product being suppressed during the Deals for You Days event.
3.  **Category Accuracy:** Ensure your product is mapped to the correct leaf category. Mis-categorizing a product to take advantage of lower commission rates or higher traffic in a different niche is a major violation that can lead to violation points.

### Fulfillment and Performance Metrics

Participation in Deals for You Days is not just about your content; it is about your ability to deliver. TikTok Shop monitors your backend performance daily.

*   **Late Dispatch Rate (LDR):** Your store's LDR over the last 30 days must be 20% or lower. If you anticipate a surge in orders during the campaign, you must scale your fulfillment operations in advance. High-volume sellers often use third-party logistics (3PL) to maintain these tight windows.
*   **[Account Health Rating](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/policy/tiktok-shop-account-health-rating-transition-guide) (AHR):** This is a point-based system. Penalties trigger at specific thresholds: 150, 100, 50, and 0 points. As your points drop, you face escalating restrictions, starting with a loss of eligibility for platform-wide promotions and ending with total account suspension.
*   **Creator Metrics:** If you are working with affiliates, their performance affects your brand. TikTok monitors creators via a daily Promotion Performance Score (PPS) and Creator [Health Rating](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/policy/tiktok-shop-account-health-rating-transition-guide) (CHR). A creator with a low CHR can negatively impact the visibility of your products on their "Showcase" tab.

### Listing Violations

The most frequent reason for a listing being rejected during a campaign is "Significantly Not As Described" (SNAD). This happens when the physical product received by the customer does not match the images or description provided.

*   **SNAD Penalties:** Shipping mismatched items results in 1 to 48 violation points. If a customer reports that a product is a different color, size, or material than what was shown in the Deals for You Days promotion, TikTok will reduce your store's visibility immediately.
*   **Unsupported Claims:** Any claim that a product can treat, cure, or prevent a disease is strictly prohibited. Similarly, exaggerated weight-loss claims ("lose 10lbs in 2 days") carry 6 to 48 violation points.
*   **Prohibited Products:** Ensure your items are not on the restricted list. This includes certain types of jewelry, supplements, and used items that require specific certifications.

### Content and Creator Violations

If you are using short-form videos or Live streams to promote your deals, you must adhere to the Content Enforcement Policy.

*   **Reproduced Content:** This is a major focus for TikTok in 2026. Posting duplicate videos from other creators or re-uploading your own content without substantial transformation triggers a 48-point penalty. This is essentially a "one-strike" policy for account suspension.
*   **Off-Platform Redirection:** Do not include links, QR codes, or verbal calls-to-action that drive users to external websites like Shopify or Amazon. TikTok wants the entire transaction to happen within the TikTok Shop ecosystem. Including a URL in your video or description will result in the content being removed.
*   **Deceptive Promotion:** If your video shows a product at a "deal" price that is not actually available in the linked product card, your content will be flagged for "Deceptive Promotion."

### How to File an Appeal

If you believe a violation was issued in error—such as an automated flag for "Reproduced Content" on a video you actually filmed—you must act quickly.

*   **Timeline:** Sellers have 30 calendar days from the date the violation was issued to file an appeal.
*   **Documentation:** You must provide clear evidence. For SNAD violations, provide high-resolution photos of the product and packaging. For listing rejections, provide supplier invoices and proof of inventory to show the item is authentic and in your possession.
*   **Fund Recovery:** If an appeal is rejected or ignored, TikTok Shop has the right to withhold your account balance for 180 days before disbursing net funds. This is a standard security measure to cover potential customer refunds or chargebacks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum Shop Performance Score for TikTok Shop campaigns?
To participate in major events like Deals for You Days, sellers generally need a Shop Performance Score (SPS) of 3.5 or higher. This score is calculated based on your product ratings, fulfillment speed, and customer service response times over the preceding 30 to 90 days.

### Can I use my Amazon product images on TikTok Shop?
Yes, but you must ensure they meet TikTok's specific specs. While Amazon recommends [2000x2000 px](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G1881), TikTok accepts anything from 600x600 to 6000x6000 pixels. The most important change is removing any Amazon-specific branding or "Best Seller" badges that might be embedded in your secondary images.

### How long do violation points stay on my TikTok Shop account?
Violation points typically stay on your record for a rolling 180-day period. If you accumulate enough points to hit a threshold (like 48 points), your account may be permanently deactivated. It is critical to appeal any "incorrect" violations immediately to keep your total point count as low as possible.

### Why was my product rejected for "Unsupported Claims"?
This usually happens if your product description contains words that TikTok's AI identifies as medical or health-related. To fix this, remove any mentions of "curing," "healing," or specific weight-loss numbers. Focus your copy on the features and materials of the product rather than the biological results.
