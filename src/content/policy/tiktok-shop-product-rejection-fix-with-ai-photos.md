## Detailed Requirements

![Detailed Requirements](/img/tiktok-shop-product-rejection-fix-with-ai-photos/inline-2.webp)


Set your camera or AI generation tool to a 1:1 aspect ratio before you begin creating assets to avoid forced cropping that cuts off essential product features. While TikTok Shop allows up to 10 images per listing, the first image—the "Main Image"—is the most heavily scrutinized.

### Primary Image Integrity
Your primary image must be a clear, unobstructed view of the product. TikTok Shop’s current policy dictates that the product should occupy between 60% and 85% of the frame. If the product is too small, the system flags it as "Low Quality." If it is too large and touches the edges of the frame, it may be rejected for "Incomplete Product Display."

### Resolution and Clarity
The 600x600 pixel minimum is a hard floor. However, as of 2026-05-23, high-performing sellers are standardizing at 1200x1200px to ensure that when a customer uses the "pinch-to-zoom" feature on the TikTok app, the textures and labels remain legible. Images that appear blurry, pixelated, or show visible compression artifacts (common when saving JPEGs at low quality) are instantly flagged by automated systems.

### Intellectual Property and Rights
Sellers must have the legal right to use every image uploaded. TikTok Shop has integrated advanced image-matching technology to identify unauthorized stock photos. If you are a dropshipper or a multi-platform reseller using the same manufacturer photos as 500 other sellers, you risk a "Duplicate Listing" or "IP Infringement" rejection. This is where AI-generated variations become critical; by creating a unique visual representation of the product, you bypass the hash-matching algorithms that flag identical file data.

### Accessory and Packaging Visibility
If your product description mentions a "3-piece set" or "includes a carrying case," those items must be visible in at least one of the secondary images. TikTok’s manual review team frequently rejects listings where the images do not match the "What's in the Box" section of the description. Ensure all accompanying accessories are clearly visible and not obscured by shadows or creative lighting.

TikTok Shop’s rejection reasons often seem vague, but they usually fall into four distinct buckets.

### 'Unqualified Image of Product'
This rejection is frequently triggered when the listing lacks the required variety of angles. For high-risk categories like Electronics or Health & Personal Care, TikTok may require up to 6 different views: front, back, top, bottom, left, and right. If you only provide three views, the listing will be suspended until the additional angles are provided. This rejection also occurs if the image does not show the actual [product packaging](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/stories/flux-1-2-pro-product-packaging-design-case-study), which TikTok uses to verify brand authenticity.

### 'Low Quality Or Non Compliant Images'
This is the most common automated flag. It covers a range of technical errors:
*   **Pixelation:** Using low-res screenshots from a mobile device.
*   **Distracting Backgrounds:** Using a "lifestyle" shot (e.g., a person holding the product in a park) as the primary image.
*   **Text Overlays:** Including "Limited Time Offer," "2026 Model," or price tags in the image.
*   **Borders:** Any black or colored bars used to force a non-square image into a 1:1 slot.

### 'Misleading Claims'
TikTok Shop’s AI scans the text on your product packaging within the image. If your product photo shows a bottle that says "Cures Insomnia" but your text description avoids medical claims to stay compliant, the image itself will trigger a rejection for "Misleading Claims." The visual and the verbal must be in 100% alignment.

### 'Duplicate Listings'
If you manage multiple TikTok Shops or have previously deleted and re-uploaded a listing, the system may recognize the image file's digital signature. Reusing the exact same image file across different SKUs—even for color variations—can trigger this flag. Each SKU should ideally have a unique primary image to avoid being caught in the "spam" filter.

### Automated vs. Manual Review Flags
Initial rejections are almost always automated. Within seconds of clicking "Submit," the computer vision API checks for resolution, background color, and text-to-image ratios. If you pass this, your listing enters a "Pending" state where a human reviewer or a more complex secondary [AI model](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/policy/etsy-ai-model-disclosure-badge-guide) checks for brand authorization and policy compliance. Manual reviews are typically completed within 24 to 48 hours, though this can extend during peak periods like Black Friday or the TikTok "Super Brand Day" events.

### IP and Brand Authorization Rejections
If you are selling a branded product (e.g., Nike, Stanley, or Sony), TikTok Shop requires you to upload "Brand Authorization" documents. If your images look like official brand assets but you haven't uploaded the corresponding certificates in the "Brand Center," your images will be rejected for "Unauthorized Use of Brand Assets." Using AI to generate custom "in-use" photos of the product can sometimes help in demonstrating that you have physical possession of the stock, which can be useful in appeal processes.
