Seeing your TikTok Shop listings throttled or rejected because of a "Low Quality Image" alert stops your sales momentum instantly. You need to align your visual assets with TikTok’s strict 2026 metadata and aesthetic standards to keep your shop active and your products discoverable.

Fix the TikTok Shop low quality image alert by ensuring photos meet the 600x600 pixel minimum and are under 5MB. Use a white background, remove watermarks, and upload at least 5 images in JPG or PNG.

## Quick Reference Table

Audit your current image folder against these technical specifications before starting your next upload batch to ensure every file passes the initial automated check.

| :--- | :--- |
| **Minimum Resolution** | 600x600 pixels |
| **Maximum File Size** | 5MB per image |
| **Accepted Formats** | JPG, JPEG, PNG |
| **Aspect Ratio** | 1:1 (Square) |
| **Image Quantity** | 1 to 9 images (5+ recommended) |
| **Main Image Background** | White or neutral |
| **Text/Watermarks** | Prohibited on main image |

## Detailed Requirements

![Detailed Requirements](/img/fix-tiktok-shop-low-quality-image-alert/inline-2.webp)


Check your "Product Optimizer" tab in the TikTok Seller Center to see which listings are currently flagged as "Fair" or "Poor" due to image quality issues. TikTok’s algorithm does not just look at whether an image exists; it evaluates the metadata and visual clarity to determine if the listing provides a "Good" user experience.

### Listing Quality Tiers

TikTok Shop categorizes [product listings](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/policy/amazon-rufus-seo-product-listings-guide) into three quality tiers: Poor, Fair, and Good. To achieve a "Good" listing quality tier, TikTok requires sellers to upload at least 5 high-resolution images per product. Listings with fewer than 5 images or those missing category-specific details are automatically downgraded to a "Fair" or "Poor" quality tier, which significantly reduces their visibility in the "Shop" tab and search results.

The "Good" tier is not just a badge; it is a prerequisite for participating in certain platform-wide flash sales and promotional events. If your images are flagged as low quality, your listing may be suppressed, meaning it won't appear to potential buyers even if your SEO keywords are perfect.

### Background and Composition Rules

The main product image—the first one a buyer sees—must clearly showcase the item on a white or neutral background, without blurred elements or placeholders. While the platform allows lifestyle images in the subsequent slots (images 2 through 9), the primary slot is reserved for a clean, professional shot.

While the absolute minimum size is 600x600 pixels, uploading higher resolution images prevents pixelation on high-density mobile screens. Modern smartphones often have screen resolutions that exceed 1000 pixels in width; an image that only meets the bare minimum might look "fuzzy" or "soft" to a user on a high-end device, leading to lower conversion rates even if the system technically accepts the file.

### Resolution Too Low
Uploading images smaller than the 600x600 pixel threshold automatically triggers the low-quality alert. This often happens when sellers take screenshots of their products from other platforms or use thumbnails instead of original source files. 

### File Size Exceeds Limits
Images larger than 5MB will fail to upload or be heavily compressed by the system. When TikTok's internal servers compress a 10MB or 15MB professional RAW export down to a web-friendly size, it often results in a blurry appearance or "artifacting" that triggers the quality alert.

### Watermarks and Overlays
TikTok Shop rejects images containing distracting logos, watermarks, or promotional text overlays on the main image. Unlike some other social platforms that allow "20% text" or small branding marks, TikTok Shop's 2026 policy is strict: the main image must be a clean representation of the product. Text like "Free Shipping" or "Best Seller" should be handled via the platform's native promotional tools, not baked into the image file.

### Wrong File Format
Attempting to upload HEIC (Apple's default) or WEBP files directly through the standard UI can cause errors or listing rejections. Stick to JPG, JPEG, or PNG to ensure the TikTok Shop parser can read the file data correctly.

### Cluttered Backgrounds
Busy or distracting backgrounds violate the platform's recommendation for clean, professional product presentation. If the background contains other products not included in the sale, or if the environment is messy, the AI-driven moderation tool may flag the image as "low quality" or "confusing to the buyer."
