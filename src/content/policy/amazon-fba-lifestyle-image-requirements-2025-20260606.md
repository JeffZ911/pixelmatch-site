# Amazon FBA Lifestyle Image Requirements 2025: The Complete Seller's Guide

As of 2025, Amazon allows lifestyle images as the main image ONLY for specific categories like [BED, BED_FRAME, MATTRESS, RUG, RUG_PAD, SOFA](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881). For all other products, main images require a pure white background, while lifestyle shots must be used as secondary images.

The [1,000 pixels on the longest side](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881) requirement is critical because it triggers the "zoom" functionality on the product detail page, which has been shown to significantly impact conversion rates. While the absolute [minimum is 500 pixels](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881), any image uploaded below the 1,000-pixel threshold is a missed opportunity for buyer engagement.

## Detailed Requirements

![Detailed Requirements](/img/amazon-fba-lifestyle-image-requirements-2025-20260606/inline-2.webp)


Set your camera or AI generation tool to a 1:1 square aspect ratio and a resolution of at least 2000x2000 pixels to future-proof your assets. High-resolution source files allow you to crop into specific product details for secondary "feature" shots without losing clarity or violating the [minimum 1,000-pixel zoom requirement](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881).

Amazon's automated systems now use advanced computer vision to detect "off-white" backgrounds. If your background is RGB 254, 254, 254, your listing may be flagged. Always verify that your main images use [pure white (RGB 255, 255, 255)](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881).

Technical specifications for 2025 include:
*   **File Size:** Keep files [under 10MB](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881) to ensure fast loading on mobile devices.
*   **Frame Filling:** The product must fill [at least 85% of the image frame](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881). This prevents "small product" syndrome where the item looks insignificant in search results.
*   **Content Restrictions:** No text, logos, borders, color blocks, or watermarks are allowed over the product or in the background of a main image.
*   **Preferred Format:** [JPEG (.jpg)](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881) is the preferred file format due to its balance of compression and quality, though TIFF, PNG, and non-animated GIFs are accepted.

### Main Image Exceptions

Amazon has acknowledged that certain products, specifically large furniture and floor coverings, look unappealing or confusing when isolated on a white background. As of 2025, if your product is classified under [BED, BED_FRAME, MATTRESS, RUG, RUG_PAD, or SOFA](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881), you are permitted to use a lifestyle image as your MAIN image. 

However, this is not a license for cluttered photography. The product must still be the clear focus. For a rug, the image should show the rug in a room setting, but the rug must not be obscured by excessive furniture. For a bed frame, the frame must be clearly visible, even if styled with bedding. If you sell in these categories, batch-generate AI product images to test different room settings (Bohemian, Modern, Industrial) to see which environment drives the highest click-through rate (CTR) while remaining compliant.

### Secondary Image Best Practices

While the main image gets the click, the secondary lifestyle images get the sale. Amazon allows up to 9 images, though only 7 are typically visible on the main desktop view. Use these slots to address customer objections and demonstrate the product in use.

For secondary lifestyle images:
1.  **The "In-Use" Shot:** Show the product being used by your target demographic. If you sell a kitchen gadget, show it in a kitchen, not just on a countertop.
2.  **The Scale Shot:** Include an image that conveys the size of the product. This can be a hand holding the item or the item placed next to a common object (like a smartphone or a standard coffee mug).
3.  **The Infographic:** Use text overlays to highlight technical specs or materials. Unlike main images, secondary images [allow text and graphics](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881) as long as they provide value to the customer.
4.  **The Multi-Platform Pivot:** If you sell on Shopify or TikTok Shop alongside Amazon, your lifestyle images can be more "social-media styled." However, ensure the Amazon version remains professional and high-resolution.

Amazon's AI-driven moderation is increasingly aggressive, often rejecting images within minutes of an upload if they fail to meet the [pure white background (RGB 255, 255, 255)](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881) standard.

The most frequent reasons for rejection in 2025 include:

*   **Lifestyle Backgrounds in Restricted Categories:** Attempting to use a lifestyle background for a main image in a category like "Electronics" or "Supplements" will result in immediate suppression.
*   **Confusing Props:** Including accessories, batteries, or decorative items that are not included in the purchase. If a customer sees a spatula next to a pan in the main image, they expect to receive the spatula.
*   **Technical Resolution Failures:** Uploading images where the resolution falls below the [500 pixels minimum](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881) requirement. This usually happens when sellers try to "upscale" a small thumbnail from a manufacturer's website.
*   **Ghost Mannequins and Packaging:** For most categories, the main image must show the product out of its packaging. While "ghost mannequins" are common in apparel, they must be executed perfectly; any visible part of the mannequin can trigger a rejection.
*   **Prohibited Overlays:** Using "Best Seller" badges, "Organic" stamps, or "Warranty" logos on the main image. Amazon views these as "promotional material" which is strictly [prohibited in main images](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881).

To avoid these issues, always perform a "before and after" check. Before uploading, use a color picker tool to ensure the background is exactly #FFFFFF. After uploading, wait 24 hours and check the mobile view of your listing to ensure the image hasn't been cropped awkwardly by Amazon's mobile responsive layout.

### Correcting Resolution and Cropping

If your images are rejected for being too small or having the product too far away, you must re-render or re-shoot the asset. Do not simply "stretch" a small image to 1,000 pixels; this creates pixelation that Amazon's "Quality Alert" system will flag.

Follow these steps to fix resolution issues:
1.  **Re-export from Source:** If you have the original RAW files or high-res AI generations, re-export them at 2000x2000 pixels.
2.  **Adjust the 85% Rule:** Use a cropping tool to ensure the product occupies [at least 85% of the frame](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G1881). If your product is long and thin (like a yoga mat), orient it diagonally to maximize frame coverage.
3.  **Check the Aspect Ratio:** Amazon prefers square 1:1 images. If you upload a 16:9 lifestyle image, Amazon will add white bars to the top and bottom, which can make your product look smaller and reduce your CTR.
