Stop guessing which AI-generated images will get your Etsy shop suspended and start auditing your listings against the 2026 disclosure mandates. Failing to distinguish between a "synthetic performer" and a real human model or mislabeling an AI-generated print as "Photography" can now trigger automated listing takedowns and permanent shop closures.

**Quick Answer:** Under Etsy's Creativity Standards, AI-generated artwork must be categorized as "Designed by a seller" and explicitly disclosed in the listing description. Additionally, a June 2026 law requires sellers using AI-generated human models in mockups to add a visible disclosure badge, and AI prompt bundles are strictly prohibited.

## Detailed Requirements

![Detailed Requirements](/img/etsy-creativity-standards-ai-images-policy/inline-2.webp)


Update your listing "About this listing" section to reflect that your AI-assisted work is "Designed by a seller" rather than "Handmade." Since the June 10, 2025 update to Etsy's Creativity Standards, the platform has moved away from a binary "Handmade or Not" system to a more nuanced categorization that tracks the level of human involvement.

### Disclosure Rules for AI Art
When you list an item that was generated using AI—such as a digital download, a poster print, or a t-shirt graphic—you must explicitly state this in the listing description. Etsy requires sellers to be transparent so buyers understand they are purchasing a product created via prompt engineering and algorithmic generation. 

To remain compliant, add a clear sentence at the beginning of your description: "This artwork was created with the assistance of AI tools and finalized by our studio." Furthermore, you must select the correct taxonomy. AI-generated images can no longer be categorized under "Photography." Instead, they must be listed under [Digital Prints or Digital Art](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/16666696670871-What-is-Etsy-s-stance-on-AI-creations-).

### Listing Image Requirements
Your primary listing image (the "Hero Image") is the most strictly regulated asset in your shop. According to Etsy’s Listing Image Requirements, the first image must feature a finished, customized item. If you are selling a physical product, such as a handmade mug or a screen-printed shirt, the first image cannot be an AI-generated mockup. It must be a photograph of the actual item you have produced.

Computer-generated mockups are only permitted in the secondary image slots (images 2 through 10). This ensures that the buyer sees the real-world quality, texture, and color of the product before they see a stylized AI representation. For digital sellers, this means your first image should be a clear representation of the file the customer will receive, not a lifestyle mockup of that file in a room that doesn't exist.

### The 2026 AI Human Model Law
As of June 9, 2026, sellers must comply with new regulations regarding "synthetic performers." If you use AI-generated humans in your lifestyle mockups—whether they are wearing your jewelry or holding your products—you are legally required to include a clear disclosure badge on the image itself. 

This disclosure, such as "AI model used" or "Digitally generated person," must be legible and placed in a position that is not obscured by Etsy's UI elements. This law aims to prevent consumer deception regarding human appearance and product fit. If you use tools like Photoroom’s Pro tier at $12.99/mo to swap models or Pebblely’s $19/mo plan to generate lifestyle scenes, you must manually add these text overlays to remain compliant with both state law and Etsy’s updated safety guidelines.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use AI to enhance my product photos on Etsy?
Yes, you can use AI to enhance backgrounds, adjust lighting, or remove imperfections from your product photos. However, you must not use AI to change the physical characteristics of the product itself (like color or texture) if it misleads the buyer about what they will receive. Using PixelMatch to generate a professional background for a real product photo is a standard and safe practice under the 2026 Creativity Standards.

### Do I need to disclose AI if I only used it for the background?
As of 2026, if the product itself is real and photographed, you generally do not need a prominent text disclosure in the description for just the background. However, if the background includes "synthetic humans" or AI-generated models, you must include a disclosure badge on the image to comply with New York's AI disclosure law and similar emerging regulations.

### Is Midjourney art allowed on Etsy?
Yes, artwork generated by Midjourney is allowed as long as it is sold as a finished product (like a digital download or a physical print) and is disclosed as "Designed by a seller." You cannot sell the prompts used to create the art, as [AI prompt bundles are strictly prohibited](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/16666696670871-What-is-Etsy-s-stance-on-AI-creations-) on the marketplace.

### What happens if I don't disclose AI usage on my Etsy shop?
Failing to disclose AI usage can lead to several penalties, including your listings being removed from search results, the deactivation of specific listings, or the permanent suspension of your shop. Etsy uses automated tools to detect AI-generated content, and they rely on buyer reports to identify shops that are not being transparent about their creative process.
