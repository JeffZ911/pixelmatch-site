### Sponsored Ads vs. Product Detail Page Specs

Differentiate your assets based on where they appear. Sponsored Brands videos are "auto-play, mute-on," meaning the first few seconds must be visually arresting without relying on sound. For these ads, Amazon mandates a <a href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=16%3A9+aspect+ratio&tag=jeffzen911-20" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" target="_blank">16:9 aspect ratio</a>. If you attempt to upload a square (1:1) video intended for Instagram, Amazon will reject it for <a href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=letterboxing+%28black+bars%29&tag=jeffzen911-20" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" target="_blank">letterboxing (black bars)</a>.

Product Detail Page (PDP) videos are more flexible but higher stakes. While they allow for [files up to 5 GB](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/G200332540), the goal is conversion, not just attention. For PDPs, 1080p is still the gold standard, but you must ensure the video does not contain "off-Amazon" references.

### Audio and Codec Requirements

Set your video export settings to <a href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=128+kbps+or+higher+for+audio&tag=jeffzen911-20" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" target="_blank">128 kbps or higher for audio</a> and a sample rate of <a href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=44.1+kHz&tag=jeffzen911-20" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" target="_blank">44.1 kHz</a>. Even though Sponsored Brands videos often play on mute, the file must contain an audio track that meets these technical specs to pass the automated validation check. 

For the video stream, use the H.264 or H.265 codec. Most [AI video](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/compare/amazon-ai-video-generator-vs-canva-magic) generators like Booth.AI or Adobe Express default to these, but if you are using a custom Stable Diffusion pipeline, you must manually verify that your <a href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=bitrate+is+at+least+8+Mbps+for+1080p+content&tag=jeffzen911-20" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" target="_blank">bitrate is at least 8 Mbps for 1080p content</a>.

### Prohibited Visual Elements

Avoid the "Uncanny Valley" of AI generation. Amazon explicitly prohibits <a href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=blurry+frames+and+simple+slideshows&tag=jeffzen911-20" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" target="_blank">blurry frames and simple slideshows</a>. If your video looks like a series of static images with a simple "Ken Burns" zoom effect, it will be rejected. The motion must be fluid and "video-like."

Furthermore, do not use <a href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=360-degree+rotational+views&tag=jeffzen911-20" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" target="_blank">360-degree rotational views</a> in Sponsored Products video ads. While these are popular on Shopify or custom sites, Amazon reserves rotational views for specific interactive widgets on the PDP, not for video ad slots. 

Common visual triggers for rejection include:
*   **Letterboxing:** Any black bars on the top, bottom, or sides.
*   **Low Resolution:** Anything appearing pixelated on a 4K screen.
*   **Emojis:** Never use emoticons, hearts, or stars in the video overlay.
*   **Branding:** Do not include logos unless they are <a href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=physically+part+of+the+product+or+packaging&tag=jeffzen911-20" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" target="_blank">physically part of the product or packaging</a>.

### Banned Text and Claims

Check your text overlays against the 2026 prohibited phrases list. Amazon's OCR (Optical Character Recognition) bots scan every frame for text. If you include <a href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=promotional+claims+like+%22Best+Seller%2C%22+%22Top+Rated%2C%22+or+pricing+details&tag=jeffzen911-20" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" target="_blank">promotional claims like "Best Seller," "Top Rated," or pricing details</a>, the video will be rejected immediately. 

Specifically, avoid any "Call to Action" (CTA) that directs users away from the platform. Phrases like "Visit our website for a discount" or "Follow us on social media" are strictly forbidden. Your text should focus entirely on product features or benefits, such as "Waterproof up to 30m" or "100% Organic Cotton."

## How to Fix Each Issue

![How to Fix Each Issue](/img/amazon-ai-video-generator-product-photos-policy/inline-4.webp)


Apply dynamic "Camera Pan" or "Zoom" effects in your AI generator to bypass the "static slideshow" rejection trigger. If you are using a tool like Pebblely or Canva to animate your product photos, ensure the transitions are not just "fades." Amazon requires the product to move or the environment to change dynamically to qualify as "video."

### Fixing Slideshow Rejections

If your video was rejected for being a "slideshow," you need to increase the temporal consistency of your AI generation. Instead of stitching together five different AI images, use a video-first AI tool like Sora or Kling (or Amazon's native generator) that treats the product as a 3D object in space.

If you don't have access to high-end video AI, use PixelMatch to generate consistent product angles. By having the exact same lighting and product position across multiple images, you can create a "stop-motion" effect that looks intentional and professional rather than a cheap slideshow. Ensure the product takes up <a href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=at+least+50%25+of+the+frame&tag=jeffzen911-20" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" target="_blank">at least 50% of the frame</a> throughout these transitions.

### Correcting Aspect Ratio and Framing

Use generative fill to expand 1:1 square images into 16:9 canvases. Many sellers make the mistake of "stretching" their 1:1 images to fit 16:9, which results in distortion. Amazon's <a href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=policy+on+image+quality&tag=jeffzen911-20" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" target="_blank">policy on image quality</a> prohibits any distorted or "squashed" visuals.

Instead, use an AI expansion tool (like those found in Adobe Express or Photoroom's Pro tier at $12.99/mo) to "outpaint" the background. This creates a native 1920x1080 frame where the product remains at the correct scale. 

**Checklist for Fixing Rejections:**
1.  **Remove Text:** Delete any "Free Shipping" or "Limited Time" text overlays.
2.  **Verify Resolution:** Ensure the export is exactly 1920x1080.
3.  **Check Audio:** Add a generic, royalty-free background track if your file is "video-only."
4.  **Re-center Product:** Ensure the product doesn't drift into the "safe zones" where Amazon overlays the "Sponsored" tag or the "Mute" button.
