## Detailed Requirements

![Detailed Requirements](/img/tiktok-shop-account-health-rating-transition-guide/inline-2.webp)


Calculate your rolling 90-day order volume to determine your point-earning potential before the transition hits. The AHR system is dynamic, meaning your score is not a static trophy but a reflection of your last three months of performance. While the legacy system punished you for mistakes, the new system rewards volume, allowing high-growth sellers to "buffer" their health rating against accidental violations.

### The 90-Day Rolling Window
TikTok now evaluates your risk profile based on a 90-day rolling period. This is a double-edged sword. While it means a "Serious" violation from six months ago no longer drags down your score, it also means you must consistently generate "Clean" orders to maintain your points. Points earned from completed, non-refunded orders only stay in your balance for 90 days. If your sales volume drops significantly, your AHR could naturally drift downward toward the 200-point baseline, leaving you with less of a safety net if a violation occurs.

### Milestone Enforcement Thresholds
Falling below the 150-point mark triggers automated enforcement actions that can cripple a multi-platform seller's revenue. TikTok Shop has standardized these milestone restrictions to scale with the severity of your score:
*   **150 Points:** 7-day restriction on joining new official campaigns and a limit on the number of new product listings you can upload daily.
*   **100 Points:** 14-day suspension from the TikTok Shop Affiliate program. Your "Shop" tab remains visible, but creators can no longer tag your products.
*   **50 Points:** 28-day restriction on all traffic-driving features, including paid ads and livestreaming capabilities.

### Image and Content Compliance
The 2026 update introduces stricter automated scanning for product listings. To maintain a "Healthy" status, every listing must feature at least 5 high-resolution images. TikTok's Listing Quality Guidelines now mandate a minimum resolution of 600x600 pixels with a strict 1:1 aspect ratio. Failure to meet these specs results in "Listing Quality" violations, which subtract points from your AHR and prevent the listing from being eligible for the "For You" feed.

### The Creator-Seller Link
If you use affiliates, monitor your Creator Health Rating (CHR) via the Affiliate Center. Under the new policy, if a creator linked to your product receives a violation for "Misleading Content" or "Shocking Imagery," your brand can receive a secondary "Affiliate Misconduct" penalty. This link ensures that brands are responsible for the creators they approve for their samples.

### Image Policy Violations
The most common reason for a sudden AHR drop is the "Main Image" violation. TikTok Shop requires the primary image to be a clear, front-facing view of the product. Using images with watermarks, promotional text (e.g., "50% OFF"), or busy backgrounds will lead to an immediate rejection. While tools like Photoroom's Pro tier at $12.99/mo can help with individual background removal, many sellers fail because they only upload 1 or 2 images. You must provide at least 5 images to satisfy the quality score requirements.

### Late Dispatch Rate (LDR)
Your AHR is heavily weighted by your ability to ship orders within the agreed Service Level Agreement (SLA). On TikTok Shop, "Late Dispatch" occurs if you do not move an order to "Awaiting Collection" or "Shipped" within the timeframe specified in your shipping template (usually 2 business days). High LDR doesn't just result in a one-time penalty; it prevents you from earning the 4 points per 200 orders needed to grow your score, as only "Completed" orders count toward your health.

### Misleading Metadata and Clickbait
TikTok’s AI now cross-references your product title with the AI-generated transcript of any linked affiliate videos. If your title claims a product is "Medical Grade" or "Permanent" but the documentation doesn't support it, you will face a "Misleading Content" violation. In the health and beauty niche, exaggerated claims are the leading cause of account deactivation. Avoid words like "cure," "miracle," or "instant weight loss," which are flagged by the Product Listing Guidelines.

### Affiliate Misconduct and Spam
If your affiliate partners use "looping" videos or "static image" livestreams to sell your products, TikTok will flag your account for "Low-Quality Content." Even if you didn't produce the video, the association with your SKU can lead to a point deduction. You must actively manage your affiliate permissions and remove creators who violate TikTok's community standards.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens if my TikTok Shop Account Health Rating hits 0?
If your AHR hits 0, your account is subject to permanent deactivation. You will lose access to the Seller Center, and any remaining funds in your account may be withheld for up to 90 days to cover potential customer returns or disputes. You have 30 days to lodge an appeal with supporting evidence (such as invoices or shipping manifests) to attempt to restore the account.

### Can I earn points back if I stop getting violations?
Yes, but simply "not making mistakes" isn't enough to reach the 1,000-point ceiling. You earn 4 points for every 200 completed orders that do not result in a violation. Because the system operates on a 90-day rolling window, you must maintain consistent sales volume to keep those earned points in your balance.

### Do customer returns affect my Account Health Rating?
Standard "Change of Mind" returns do not negatively impact your AHR. However, "Seller-Fault" returns—such as those citing "Product Not as Described" or "Wrong Item Sent"—can trigger a "Product Quality" violation. If your Seller-Fault Return Rate exceeds the category benchmark, TikTok will deduct points from your AHR and may temporarily hide the listing from the "Shop" tab.

### Is the 600x600 pixel requirement mandatory for all images?
Yes, TikTok Shop's Listing Quality Guidelines state that all images must be at least 600x600 pixels. Images smaller than this are often automatically rejected during the upload process or result in a "Low Quality" flag that suppresses your product's visibility in search results.
