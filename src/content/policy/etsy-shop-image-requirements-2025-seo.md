# Etsy Shop Image Requirements for 2025 SEO

For 2025 and 2026, Etsy recommends listing images be at least [2000 x 2000 pixels](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015663347-Requirements-and-Best-Practices-for-Images-in-Your-Etsy-Shop) and under 1MB in size. Supported formats are JPG, PNG, GIF, SVG, and HEIC, but transparent PNGs will render with a black background.

## Detailed Requirements

![Detailed Requirements](/img/etsy-shop-image-requirements-2025-seo/inline-2.webp)


Run a batch export of your product catalog using a 1:1 or 4:3 aspect ratio to ensure your items are not cropped awkwardly in the search results. While Etsy’s system can handle multiple formats, the platform’s 2026 rendering engine is optimized for high-resolution JPGs that balance clarity with load speed.

Etsy currently supports [.jpg, .gif, .png, .svg, or .heic](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015663347-Requirements-and-Best-Practices-for-Images-in-Your-Etsy-Shop) file formats. However, you must avoid using transparent .png files for your listings. Because Etsy does not support transparency in the listing viewer, any transparent areas in your file will [appear as solid black](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015663347-Requirements-and-Best-Practices-for-Images-in-Your-Etsy-Shop), which often obscures product details and leads to immediate customer bounce.

### Listing Image SEO Best Practices

Add descriptive alt text to every image you upload. Etsy’s search algorithm has evolved to use computer vision and AI to "read" the contents of your photos. By adding alt text, you provide a secondary text signal that reinforces your keywords. For example, instead of "Product_Photo_1," use "Handmade ceramic coffee mug with blue glaze and ergonomic handle."

Furthermore, the primary listing photo must be a [singular image of a finished product](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015663347-Requirements-and-Best-Practices-for-Images-in-Your-Etsy-Shop). Etsy’s search visibility guidelines suggest that collages (multiple images stitched into one file) are often deprioritized in search because they appear cluttered on mobile devices. For custom items, Etsy’s Seller Policy requires that the first image feature a finished, customized item rather than a blank mockup or a digital placeholder. This builds buyer trust and ensures you are compliant with the "Creativity Standards" updated in late 2024.

### Shop Banners and Branding

Switch to a "Big Banner" if you want to highlight a specific promotion or seasonal collection. While the Mini Banner is efficient for mobile users, the [1600 x 400 px Big Banner](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015663347-Requirements-and-Best-Practices-for-Images-in-Your-Etsy-Shop) provides more real estate to communicate your brand's value proposition. Ensure your Shop Icon is a clean, high-contrast version of your logo, as it is scaled down to small sizes in the Etsy app and on mobile web browsers.

Check your browser console if an upload fails; usually, the culprit is a file size exceeding [1MB](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015663347-Requirements-and-Best-Practices-for-Images-in-Your-Etsy-Shop). Large files cause timeouts on Etsy's servers, particularly during peak shopping hours. If your internet connection is unstable, even a 2MB file may fail to process correctly.

Another frequent issue is the "Black Background Glitch." As noted, this occurs when a seller uploads a PNG with a transparent background. To fix this, you must flatten the image against a solid white or lifestyle background before uploading. 

Search visibility penalties are harder to spot because they don't result in an "error" message. However, if your primary listing photo is [under 635 pixels wide](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015663347-Requirements-and-Best-Practices-for-Images-in-Your-Etsy-Shop), Etsy’s system cannot generate the zoom-in feature for customers. This lack of detail often results in lower conversion rates and a gradual drop in search rankings.

Finally, be wary of using stock photos. Etsy’s Listing Image Requirements explicitly state that for handmade items, the photos must be of the actual item you made or a representative example of the item the buyer will receive. Using generic stock photos or unedited manufacturer renderings for non-supply items can lead to shop suspension or listing deactivation.
