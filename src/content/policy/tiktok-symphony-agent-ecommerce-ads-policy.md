## Detailed Requirements

![Detailed Requirements](/img/tiktok-symphony-agent-ecommerce-ads-policy/inline-2.webp)


Configure your design software or AI generation tools to a default 1080 x 1920 px workspace to ensure your assets are TikTok-ready from the first click. TikTok’s algorithm prioritizes native-feeling, vertical content; horizontal videos with black bars (letterboxing) are frequently deprioritized or rejected for "Poor User Experience."

### Image and Video Specs

TikTok is uncompromisingly vertical. While the platform technically supports square and landscape formats, the Symphony Agent is optimized for the 9:16 aspect ratio. If you are repurposing product photography from Amazon or Shopify—which typically use 1:1 or 4:3 ratios—you must extend the background or crop the image to 1080 x 1920 px before importing it into the Symphony Agent pipeline.

Beyond dimensions, pay attention to the "Safe Zone." TikTok overlays UI elements like the "Shop Now" button, your profile icon, and the ad caption over the bottom and right side of the video. Ensure your AI-generated product or avatar is centered to avoid having critical visual information obscured by the platform's interface.

### AI Labeling and Disclosure Rules

As of 2026, TikTok has automated much of the disclosure process for internal tools, but the responsibility for transparency remains with the seller. All content generated by Symphony Agent that meets the requirements for disclosing AI use is automatically labeled as "AI-generated" in the video output. 

However, if you use an external tool like PixelMatch to generate a product background and then upload that "static" image to Symphony for video generation, you must ensure the final output retains this labeling. TikTok’s policy requires disclosure whenever AI is used to create or significantly alter "realistic" content. Failure to disclose AI-generated humans or environments can result in a permanent shadowban or ad account suspension under the TikTok Advertising Policies.

### Policy Violations in AI Outputs

Symphony Agent has built-in safety filters designed to catch violations before you even spend a dollar. Non-compliant images and text are blocked before generation within the [Creative Studio](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/stories/amazon-creative-studio-ai-image-editing-guide). This includes attempts to generate content related to:
*   **Restricted Products:** Alcohol, tobacco, or adult products.
*   **Medical Claims:** Ecommerce brands in the health and wellness space face the highest rejection rates. If your AI agent makes unverified medical or health claims (e.g., "this tea cures insomnia"), the ad will be flagged.
*   **Deceptive Content:** Any AI output that mimics a news broadcast or an official government announcement is strictly prohibited.

### Technical Spec Failures

Feeding low-resolution or improperly cropped product images into Symphony Agent can result in distorted avatars or visual artifacts. If the AI model has to "hallucinate" too much detail from a pixelated source image, the resulting video will often fail manual review for "Low Quality Creative." 

Additionally, ads are frequently rejected if the discount or offer in the AI-generated video contradicts the text in the ad caption. For example, if your PixelMatch-generated background features a "30% Off" sticker but your TikTok Ads Manager caption says "Save 50%," the platform will flag this as a "Mismatched Promotional Claim."

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does TikTok Symphony Agent cost extra for sellers?
As of mid-2026, Symphony Agent is integrated directly into the TikTok Ads Manager and Symphony [Creative Studio](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/stories/amazon-creative-studio-ai-image-editing-guide). While there is no separate subscription fee for the tool itself, the cost is effectively bundled into your overall ad spend and the resources required to manage the creative pipeline.

### Can I remove the "AI-generated" label from my TikTok ads?
No. TikTok’s policy is strict regarding AI disclosure. If the content was generated using Symphony's AI tools, the label is automatically applied and cannot be removed. Attempting to crop out or hide the label with stickers is a violation of the Ad Format and Functionality policy and can lead to account suspension.

### Why is Symphony Agent blocking my prompt?
Symphony Agent uses real-time safety filters. If your prompt includes prohibited keywords related to medical claims, weight loss, or restricted substances, the system will block the generation before it starts. Review the TikTok Advertising Policies to ensure your script and product category are eligible for AI-assisted creative.

### How do I ensure my PixelMatch images work with Symphony?
To get the best results, export your images from PixelMatch in the 1080 x 1920 px vertical format. When you upload these to Symphony Agent, choose the "Custom Background" option. This allows the Symphony AI to focus its processing power on the avatar and animation rather than trying to fix a low-resolution or incorrectly sized background.
