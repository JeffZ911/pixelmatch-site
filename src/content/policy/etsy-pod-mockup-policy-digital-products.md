## Configure your "Designed by" attributes to meet Etsy's June 2025 standards

![Configure your "Designed by" attributes to meet Etsy's June 2025 standards](/img/etsy-pod-mockup-policy-digital-products/inline-2.webp)


Apply the "Designed by a seller" category correctly to avoid your shop being flagged for resale violations. Under the June 2025 update, Etsy clarified that both Print-on-Demand (POD) and digital products belong in this category, provided the seller is the creator of the original design. This policy shift means that simply buying a pre-made mockup and slapping a basic font on it may no longer qualify as "original design" if the design lacks sufficient creative input.

### Print-on-Demand (POD) Mockup Rules

Verify that your POD listings utilize mockups that reflect the actual product your production partner provides. If you sell a Bella+Canvas 3001 t-shirt through Printify, your mockup should ideally represent that specific cut and fit. 

The 2026 enforcement rules strictly prohibit using a blank mockup with "Your Text Here" or "[Your Logo]" as the primary listing image. This is categorized as a "misleading placeholder." You must display a finished example of your design as the thumbnail. While you can include a "How to Order" slide with customization instructions as a secondary image, the first image must be a completed visual representation.

### Digital Product Mockup Rules

Distinguish your digital downloads from physical goods using explicit visual cues. Etsy’s 2026 policy requires digital sellers to ensure buyers do not mistakenly purchase a digital file expecting a physical shipment. 

For wall art, your mockup should show the art in a frame, but you must include a text overlay or a clear badge stating "Digital Download Only" or "No Physical Item Shipped." If you use AI-generated rooms for your mockups, ensure the lighting and perspective of your design match the environment. Discrepancies in shadows or perspective can lead to buyer complaints and "Item Not as Described" cases, which are now weighted more heavily in Etsy’s 2026 Search Analytics.

### AI Mockup Guidelines

Disclose your use of AI-generated assets if the *design itself* was created using AI tools. However, if you are using AI purely to generate the mockup environment (the background, the model, or the room), Etsy does not currently require a specific "AI-generated" tag for the image itself, provided the mockup is an accurate representation of the final product. 

The 2026 rules emphasize "representative accuracy." If an AI-generated mockup shows a level of detail, texture, or fabric quality that the actual POD product cannot achieve (e.g., a hyper-realistic embroidery texture on a screen-printed shirt), the listing is considered deceptive. Scan your shop for "placeholder fatigue" — a common reason for listing deactivations in 2026. The most frequent rejection reasons include:
1.  **Placeholder Text:** Using "Your Design Here" as the main thumbnail for customizable products.
2.  **Missing Disclosures:** Forgetting to list production partners like Printful, Gooten, or Gelato in the shop settings.
3.  **Low-Resolution Assets:** Uploading 1000px images that appear blurry on 4K monitors and mobile retina displays.
4.  **IP Violations:** Using mockups that feature trademarked items in the background (e.g., a Nike shoe or a Starbucks cup) that you do not have the rights to display.
5.  **Misleading Scale:** Showing a digital print in a mockup where the frame size is disproportionately large compared to the actual file resolution provided (e.g., showing a 300dpi 8x10 file as a floor-to-ceiling mural).

### Fixing Customization Placeholders

Replace every "Your Text Here" thumbnail with a high-converting example. If you sell personalized bridesmaid shirts, your primary image should show a popular name (e.g., "Sarah") in the actual font and color offered. 

1.  Export a high-resolution PNG of your best-selling customization.
2.  Generate a mockup using a unique background to stand out from competitors.
3.  Add a small "Customizable" badge in the corner (less than 10% of the image area) to signal the product's nature without cluttering the design.

### Clarifying Digital vs. Physical

Eliminate buyer confusion by standardizing your digital product badges. 
*   **Step 1:** Create a 400x400px "Digital Download" icon.
*   **Step 2:** Place this icon in the same corner of every primary mockup.
*   **Step 3:** Ensure the listing description's first line explicitly states: "This is a digital product. No physical item will be mailed."
*   **Step 4:** Set the Etsy "Type" attribute to "Digital" to ensure the automated "Instant Download" badge appears on the listing.
