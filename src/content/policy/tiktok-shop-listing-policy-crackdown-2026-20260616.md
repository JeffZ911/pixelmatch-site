## Detailed Requirements

Audit your Seller Center "Growth" tab immediately to identify which listings fall into the "Poor" or "Fair" categories. TikTok Shop now uses a three-tier quality system to determine which products receive algorithmic preference in the "Shop" tab and which are suppressed from search results.

### Listing Quality Tiers Explained

TikTok Shop categorizes every product into three distinct Listing Quality Tiers: Poor, Fair, and Good. 

1.  **Poor:** These listings are at high risk of being "Frozen." They typically feature fewer than 3 images, low-resolution files (under 600px), or missing mandatory attributes like brand or material.
2.  **Fair:** These listings are visible but receive lower search weight. They usually meet the minimum image count but fail on background consistency or lack a comprehensive product description.
3.  **Good:** To achieve the "Good" tier, you must provide at least 5 high-resolution images and complete all "Optional" attribute fields. "Good" listings receive the highest organic reach and are eligible for platform-wide flash sales.

### Main Image vs. Supplemental Images

Your primary image (the first slot in your listing) is subject to the most intense scrutiny. TikTok's 2026 policy mandates pure white backgrounds (#FFFFFF) for the main image to pass automated background checks. This is not a suggestion; the AI risk scoring system specifically looks for off-white, light gray, or textured backgrounds and flags them as non-compliant.

Supplemental images (slots 2 through 9) offer more creative freedom. This is where you should include:
*   **Lifestyle Shots:** The product in use in a real-world setting.
*   **Infographics:** Sizing charts or feature callouts (though these must be clean and professional).
*   **Scale Images:** The product next to a common object to show size.

### AI Risk Scoring in 2026

The "Frozen" or "Under Review" status is often the result of a high AI risk score. This score is generated the moment you hit "Submit" or "Update." The algorithm analyzes pixel patterns to detect:
*   **Background Inconsistency:** Even a subtle gradient or a shadow that isn't perfectly white can trigger automated background check failures. 
*   **Restricted Content:** The AI scans for medical claims, prohibited logos, or "before and after" imagery that violates TikTok’s health and safety policies.
*   **Clickbait Elements:** Any watermarks, promotional text, or price callouts (e.g., "50% OFF" or "Free Shipping" stickers) embedded in the image will result in an immediate rejection.

### The "Unqualified Image" Flag

One of the most frustrating 2026 errors is the "Unqualified Image of Product" flag. This occurs when a seller fails to provide enough visual data for the AI to verify the product's physical characteristics. For high-risk categories like Electronics, Beauty, and Fashion, TikTok now expects to see six different views: front, back, top, bottom, left, and right. If you only upload two or three photos, the system assumes the listing is "low quality" or potentially fraudulent, leading to a "Frozen" status.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the fastest way to unfreeze a TikTok Shop listing?
The fastest way is to identify the specific violation in the "Product Diagnostic" tab, fix the image (usually by removing text or changing the background to #FFFFFF), and re-submit. Most AI-based re-reviews take between 2 to 24 hours. If the automated review fails again, you must submit a manual appeal with raw, unedited photos of the product.

### Can I use the same images for TikTok Shop and Amazon?
Yes, but you must ensure they meet the stricter of the two policies. While Amazon allows for some flexibility in secondary images, TikTok Shop’s 2026 AI risk scoring is much more sensitive to "promotional text" and "watermarks." If your Amazon images have "BPA Free" or "Made in USA" badges embedded in the graphics, you must remove them for TikTok Shop to avoid a listing freeze.

### Why was my listing rejected for "Unqualified Image" even though the photo is high-quality?
This usually means you haven't provided enough angles of the product. For many categories in 2026, TikTok requires a comprehensive view of the item. Ensure you have uploaded at least five images showing the front, back, and sides of the product to satisfy the "Listing Quality" algorithm.

### Does the #FFFFFF background rule apply to all images?
No, the pure white background (#FFFFFF) is only mandatory for the **Main Image** (the first image in your listing). Supplemental images can feature lifestyle backgrounds, infographics, or different colors, provided they do not contain prohibited text or watermarks.
