### Setting Up the 1080x1920 Canvas

Start by setting your canvas to exactly 1080x1920 pixels. While 720p is technically allowed, the 2026 TikTok interface is optimized for high-density displays. Using a lower resolution results in "fuzzy" text overlays that signal a low-quality brand to potential buyers.

If you are starting with 1:1 Amazon or Shopify catalog images, do not simply stretch them. Instead, use an editor that offers "Generative Expand" or AI background generation. This allows you to place your square product photo in the center and let the AI "paint" the rest of the 9:16 environment, creating a seamless lifestyle shot that looks like it was originally photographed in a vertical studio.

### Designing for the Safe Zone

The most common mistake sellers make is ignoring the "Safe Zone." TikTok overlays a significant amount of UI elements on top of your image, including:
*   **Top 150 pixels:** Occupied by the "Following" and "For You" tabs.
*   **Bottom 450 pixels:** Occupied by the product caption, your handle, and the high-contrast "Shop" or "Buy" button.
*   **Right 120 pixels:** Occupied by the Like, Comment, and Share icons.

Design within the central 1080x1350 area. Leave a 120-pixel gutter on all four sides to prevent link stickers and product tags from overlapping your product. If your text overlay sits in the bottom 450 pixels, it will be unreadable behind the white text of your caption.

### Exporting for Photo Mode

Export your images as JPG or PNG files under the 20MB limit. For TikTok Shop, PNG is often preferred for graphics-heavy images (like those with text callouts) because it avoids the compression artifacts that can plague JPGs. If you are uploading a carousel (Photo Mode), ensure every image in the set is 9:16. If you mix 1:1 and 9:16 images, TikTok will auto-crop slides 2 through 10 to match the aspect ratio of the first slide, which can ruin your framing and cut off your call-to-action.

## FAQ

![FAQ](/img/best-9-16-image-editor-tiktok-shop/inline-5.webp)


### What is the exact pixel size for a 9:16 TikTok Shop image?
For optimal clarity in 2026, use **1080x1920 pixels**. While the platform can accept lower resolutions like 720x1280, the 1080p standard ensures your product looks sharp on all mobile devices and prevents the "blur" effect during high-speed scrolling.

### Can I use 1:1 images on TikTok Shop?
Yes, you can use 1:1 images (minimum 600x600 px) for the main product catalog listings that appear in the "Shop" tab. However, for Shoppable Videos and Photo Mode carousels—which are the primary ways users discover products in 2026—9:16 is highly recommended. 1:1 images in the feed often appear with blurred borders or are auto-cropped, leading to lower click-through rates.

### Does Stripe process TikTok Shop payments?
TikTok Shop typically uses its own native payment processing for transactions occurring within the app. However, if you are a multi-platform seller driving TikTok traffic to an external Shopify or BigCommerce store, standard Stripe fees will apply. As of 2026-05-19, the standard Stripe fee for US online card payments is **2.9% + 30¢ per transaction**. Always calculate these fees into your TikTok Ad spend ROI to ensure your net margins remain healthy.

### What is the "Safe Zone" for TikTok Shop images?
The Safe Zone is the area of your 9:16 image that is not covered by TikTok's User Interface (UI). To ensure your product is visible, keep all essential elements within the central **1080x1350 pixel** block. Specifically, avoid placing text or products in the top 150 pixels and the bottom 450 pixels.

### Why is my 9:16 image blurry after upload?
Blurriness usually stems from one of three issues:
1.  **Low Resolution:** You uploaded an image smaller than 720x1280.
2.  **File Compression:** You uploaded a file larger than 20MB, and TikTok's aggressive compression engine throttled the quality.
3.  **Wrong Aspect Ratio:** If you upload a 4:5 or 3:4 image, TikTok may upscale and crop it, losing pixel density in the process. Stick to 1080x1920 PNG files for the best results.
