## Step-by-Step Walkthrough

![Step-by-Step Walkthrough](/img/top-ai-image-generators-tiktok-shop-sellers/inline-2.webp)


Run a calibration shoot before you ever open an AI tool. Use a smartphone with a tripod to capture a "clean" product shot against a neutral, uncluttered background. This base image is the foundation for your AI generation; if the product is blurry or poorly lit in the original photo, the AI will struggle to maintain product integrity, leading to a "Misleading Content" strike from TikTok's moderation team.

### 1. Capture the Base Image
Position your product in the center of the frame. Use natural light or a simple LED ring light to ensure there are no harsh shadows that obscure the product's texture. TikTok's [1.2.1] anti-misleading policy specifically forbids AI from altering the physical appearance, color, or size of the product. Your base photo must accurately represent what the customer will receive.

### 2. Remove the Background
Upload your base photo to an AI generator to strip the background. For TikTok Shop, your primary image should ideally be a clean, white-background shot to ensure it displays correctly in the "Shop" tab and search results.

### 3. Generate Lifestyle Scenes
Use the "Product-to-Lifestyle" feature to place your product in an environment that resonates with TikTok's demographic. For example, if you are selling a portable blender, generate a scene of the product on a modern kitchen counter or in a gym bag. Ensure the AI does not add "hallucinated" features to the product, such as extra buttons or a different logo.

### 4. Apply 2026 Compliance Checks
Before exporting, verify that the product's scale relative to the background is realistic. TikTok's automated moderation frequently flags images where products appear significantly larger or smaller than their actual dimensions.

### 5. Final Export and Compression
Resize the final output to exactly 800x800 pixels. Use a 1:1 square aspect ratio. Ensure the final file size is under 5MB. If your AI tool exports in a high-resolution format like TIFF, convert it to JPEG or PNG to ensure compatibility with the TikTok Seller Center.

**Before/After Transformation Example:**
*   **Before:** A raw smartphone photo of a skincare bottle on a cluttered office desk with yellow lighting.
*   **After:** A crisp, 800x800px image of the same bottle placed on a marble bathroom vanity with soft, natural morning light, exported as a 1.2MB PNG with no distortion to the label text.

## FAQ

![FAQ](/img/top-ai-image-generators-tiktok-shop-sellers/inline-5.webp)


**Does TikTok Shop allow AI-generated images?**
Yes, TikTok Shop allows AI-generated images provided they do not deceive the buyer. You must not use AI to change the product's color, size, or features. Additionally, if the image is significantly altered (like a full lifestyle scene), you must use the "AI-generated" content toggle in the Seller Center to remain compliant with the 2026 AIGC policy.

**What is the best image size for TikTok Shop?**
The recommended size is 800x800 pixels with a 1:1 square aspect ratio. While the platform accepts images as small as 600x600 pixels, anything smaller than 800x800 pixels will result in a lower Listing [Quality Score](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/policy/etsy-search-quality-score-image-requirements-2026) and may appear blurry on high-resolution smartphone screens.

**How many images should I upload to TikTok Shop?**
You should upload a minimum of 5 images, though the platform allows up to 9. Listings with 5 or more images are much more likely to achieve a "Good" quality rating, which is a prerequisite for high organic reach in the TikTok Shop tab.

**Can I use AI to show a product in use?**
Yes, but you must be careful. If you are selling a "Before and After" product, using AI to generate the "After" result is a violation of the Misleading Content policy. Use AI only to change the background or environment (lifestyle scenes), not the results of the product itself.

**What file formats does TikTok Shop support?**
TikTok Shop supports JPEG, JPG, and PNG formats. The maximum file size per image is 5MB. For the best balance of quality and load speed, aim for a file size between 500KB and 1.5MB.
