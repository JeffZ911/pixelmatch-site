## Step-by-Step Walkthrough

![Step-by-Step Walkthrough](/img/how-to-make-pod-mockups-for-etsy-prints/inline-2.webp)


Creating a professional POD mockup requires more than just slapping a logo on a shirt. You must replicate the physical reality of the product your customer will receive.

### Matching Your POD Provider's Blanks
The most common mistake is using a generic t-shirt mockup when you are actually selling a specific brand. A Bella+Canvas 3001 has a fitted, side-seamed construction and a specific collar width that looks nothing like a heavy, tubular Gildan 5000. If your mockup shows a premium, soft-drape shirt but the customer receives a boxy, stiff garment, you are inviting a one-star review. Always check your POD provider’s dashboard (e.g., Printful, Printify, or Gooten) to identify the exact brand and model number of the blank. Search for mockups specifically tagged with that model name to ensure the texture and fit are 100% accurate.

### Preparing Your Design File
Your design must be high-resolution to avoid pixelation on high-density displays.
1.  **Resolution:** Export your design at 300 DPI (dots per inch).
2.  **Background:** Use a transparent background (.png) to ensure no "white box" appears around your art [1.1.1].
3.  **Color Space:** Convert your design to sRGB. While printers use CMYK, Etsy’s web servers display images in sRGB. Uploading CMYK files often results in "neon" or distorted colors on screen.

### Applying Realistic Shadows and Textures
A flat PNG placed on top of a photo looks fake. To make it look "printed," you must account for the fabric's highlights and shadows.
*   **Opacity:** Reduce the design layer opacity to 95-98% to let a tiny amount of the fabric texture peek through.
*   **Displacement Maps:** In tools like Photoshop or Affinity Photo, use a displacement map to warp the design into the wrinkles of the shirt.
*   **Blending Modes:** Set your design layer to "Multiply" or "Linear Burn" if you are placing a dark design on a light shirt. For light designs on dark shirts, use "Screen" or "Lighten" for the highlight areas.

### Etsy Image Size Requirements for 2026
Etsy’s technical requirements have tightened to improve mobile load speeds across the platform.
*   **Dimensions:** Aim for 2000px on the shortest side.
*   **File Size:** Keep files under 1MB. Images larger than this are compressed by Etsy’s servers, often resulting in "artifacts" or blurriness.
*   **Format:** JPG is preferred for photos; PNG is acceptable but often results in larger file sizes.

**Actionable Step:** Create a "Master Export Preset" in your design software. Set the dimensions to 2500px x 2000px, 72 DPI, sRGB color mode, and set a file size limit of 900KB. This ensures every mockup you produce is optimized for Etsy’s 2026 upload engine without further manual tweaking.

### Violating Etsy's Mockup Policy
Etsy’s Seller Policy Section 2.A.2 states that the first image in your listing must be a photo of the finished item for sale or a realistic representation of it. You cannot use a photo of a blank t-shirt or a digital file of the artwork alone as your primary image. The mockup must show the design *on* the product.

### Misrepresenting the Product
AI-generated mockups are a powerful tool, but they often hallucinate details. If your AI tool generates a mockup with a V-neck but you are selling a crew neck, you are misrepresenting your inventory. Always audit AI outputs for "anatomical correctness"—extra fingers, floating hair, or distorted fabric patterns are immediate red flags for savvy Etsy shoppers.

### Ignoring Safe Zones
Because Etsy's dynamic cropping can turn your 5:4 image into a 1:1 square in the shop view, you must keep all critical information (the design and any "Limited Edition" badges) within the center square of the image. Anything placed near the extreme left or right edges will disappear when the thumbnail is resized.

**Actionable Step:** Run a "compression audit." Take your current highest-selling listing's main image and run it through an image compressor like TinyPNG. If the file size drops by more than 40% without losing visible quality, your original was too heavy. Re-upload the compressed version to shave milliseconds off your page load time, which is a confirmed Etsy SEO ranking factor.

## FAQ

![FAQ](/img/how-to-make-pod-mockups-for-etsy-prints/inline-5.webp)


**Are POD mockups allowed on Etsy?**
Yes, mockups are fully permitted on Etsy. However, your primary (first) image must be a realistic representation of the final product. Using a blank product or a mockup that looks significantly different from the item your POD partner ships can result in listing removal.

**What is the best image size for Etsy in 2026?**
The optimal size is 2000px on the shortest side. This provides enough resolution for Etsy’s "Zoom" feature while keeping the file size small enough for fast mobile loading. Always keep the file size under 1MB.

**Can I use AI-generated mockups?**
Yes, AI-generated mockups are allowed as long as they accurately reflect the physical item. If the AI adds features (like a pocket or a specific hem style) that the actual product doesn't have, you are in violation of Etsy's Misrepresentation Policy.

**Does Etsy prefer square or rectangular images?**
Etsy recommends a 4:3 ratio, but because of their dynamic cropping system, you should treat the center 1:1 square as your "safe zone." This ensures your product looks great in both the square shop view and the rectangular search view.

**Should I use a watermark on my mockups?**
While watermarks can prevent "listing theft" by other sellers, they often distract buyers and can interfere with Etsy’s off-site ads (Google Shopping often rejects watermarked images). If you use one, keep it small, low-opacity, and away from the main product design.
