## Why This Matters for Sellers

![Why This Matters for Sellers](/img/how-to-create-amazon-a-plus-content-images/inline-1.webp)


Calculate your current unit session percentage in Amazon Seller Central before implementing A+ Content to establish a baseline for your conversion lift. Amazon A+ Content—formerly known as Enhanced Brand Content (EBC)—is not just a cosmetic upgrade; it is a conversion engine. By replacing standard text descriptions with rich media, modules, and comparison charts, sellers typically see a conversion rate increase of up to 20%. 

This space acts as your brand's mini-landing page. It allows you to overcome specific buyer objections that bullet points cannot address, such as showing the scale of a product next to a common household object or demonstrating a complex three-step assembly process. In a marketplace where "social proof" and "brand trust" are the primary currencies, a professionally designed A+ section signals that you are a legitimate brand rather than a fly-by-night reseller.

Mobile optimization is the most critical factor in 2026. With over 60% of Amazon traffic originating from mobile devices, your A+ Content images must be legible on a five-inch screen. If your text overlays are too small or your lifestyle images are cluttered, mobile shoppers will bounce. Premium A+ Content takes this further by offering interactive modules like hover hotspots and video loops, though this requires you to be Brand Registered and have an active Brand Story across your entire catalog.

## Step-by-Step Walkthrough

![Step-by-Step Walkthrough](/img/how-to-create-amazon-a-plus-content-images/inline-2.webp)


Verify your Brand Registry status in the Amazon Brand Registry portal before you begin designing assets, as approval for new brands can take 7 to 14 business days. Once you are confirmed, follow these steps to build your modules.

### Choosing the Right Modules
Amazon provides a library of modules, but you should prioritize those that answer the "Why buy?" question immediately. The "Standard Image Header with Text" is your billboard; use it for your strongest lifestyle shot. The "Standard Comparison Chart" is the most powerful module for cross-selling, allowing you to link to other products in your catalog and prevent customers from clicking away to a competitor’s listing. For technical products, use the "Standard Four Images & Text" to highlight specific components or materials.

### Formatting to 2026 Image Specs
Amazon’s 2026 requirements for A+ Content are strict. If a single image exceeds the file size or falls below the resolution threshold, the entire A+ submission will be flagged. Use high-resolution exports but compress them to stay under the 2 MB limit.

**2026 Amazon A+ Content Image Specs**

| Module Type | Required Dimensions (Pixels) | Max File Size | Format |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Header** | 970 x 600 px | 2 MB | JPG, PNG, BMP |
| **Standard Comparison** | 150 x 300 px (per image) | 2 MB | JPG, PNG, BMP |
| **Standard Single Image** | 970 x 300 px | 2 MB | JPG, PNG, BMP |
| **Standard Quad Module** | 220 x 220 px (per image) | 2 MB | JPG, PNG, BMP |
| **Standard Side Image** | 300 x 300 px | 2 MB | JPG, PNG, BMP |

*Note: All images must be in RGB color mode. CMYK files will result in distorted colors or upload errors.*

### Writing Compliant Alt-Text
Every image requires "Image Keywords" (alt-text). This is not a place for keyword stuffing. Amazon’s accessibility guidelines require alt-text to describe the image for visually impaired shoppers using screen readers. Instead of "best organic dog treats healthy snacks," use "Golden Retriever eating a bone-shaped organic chicken treat on a wooden floor." This improves your SEO footprint while remaining compliant with Amazon’s anti-spam policies.

Run a "Find" command (Ctrl+F) on your A+ Content draft for the words "Best," "Guaranteed," or "Top-rated" before hitting submit. Amazon’s automated validation bot will instantly reject any module containing these prohibited superlatives.

### Prohibited Promotional Language
Amazon prohibits any language that attempts to direct the customer away from the platform or create a sense of false urgency. You cannot include:
*   Pricing or promotional details (e.g., "Only $19.99" or "Sale price").
*   Shipping details (e.g., "Free shipping" or "Fast delivery").
*   Contact information or QR codes.
*   Time-sensitive claims (e.g., "Voted #1 product of 2025").
*   Direct mentions of competitors or their products.

### Formatting and Size Errors
Blurry images are the leading cause of manual rejection by the Amazon review team. While the minimum requirement is 72 dpi, 2026 display standards for high-density mobile screens (Retina/OLED) make 150 dpi the practical floor for professional listings. If you upscale a small image to meet the 970-pixel width requirement, the resulting pixelation will trigger a rejection for "low quality." Always start with a source image that is at least 2000 pixels wide before cropping.

### Policy Violations (Warranties & Competitors)
You cannot mention "Manufacturer Warranty" unless you are the manufacturer and provide the specific terms within the text. Furthermore, using "As seen on TV" or "Award-winning" without providing the specific date, name of the award, and the awarding body will lead to a policy violation. If your A+ Content is rejected three times for the same violation, Amazon may temporarily suspend your ability to edit A+ Content for that ASIN.

## FAQ

![FAQ](/img/how-to-create-amazon-a-plus-content-images/inline-5.webp)


**What is the maximum file size for Amazon A+ images?**
The maximum file size is 2 MB per image. If your file is larger, use a tool like TinyPNG or an AI compressor to reduce the size without sacrificing the 72+ dpi resolution required for clarity.

**Which file formats does Amazon accept for A+ Content?**
Amazon accepts JPG, PNG, and BMP formats. JPG is the recommended format for lifestyle images as it provides the best balance between file size and visual quality, leading to faster page load times for shoppers on slow mobile connections.

**How long does Amazon take to approve A+ Content?**
Standard approval time is within 7 business days. However, during peak seasons like Q4 (October through December), manual reviews can take up to 14 business days. Plan your content updates at least three weeks before major sales events like Prime Day or Black Friday.

**Can I use A+ Content if I am not Brand Registered?**
No. A+ Content is an exclusive benefit for sellers who have a verified trademark and are enrolled in the Amazon Brand Registry program. If you are not registered, your listing will only show the basic text-only product description.

**Does A+ Content help with Amazon SEO?**
While the text within A+ Content images (the alt-text) is indexed by Amazon and Google, the text in the modules themselves is generally not used for Amazon's internal search ranking. The SEO benefit is indirect: higher conversion rates and lower bounce rates signal to Amazon's A9 algorithm that your product is relevant, which improves your organic ranking over time.
