*   **Top:** Avoid the top 120px (where search and "Following/For You" tabs sit).

## FAQ

![FAQ](/img/tiktok-shop-vertical-hero-shot-editor-2026/inline-5.webp)


**What is the best aspect ratio for TikTok Shop images in 2026?**
For your standard [product listings](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/policy/amazon-rufus-seo-product-listings-guide) (the catalog itself), a 1:1 (square) ratio is required. However, for Shoppable Photos, Video Shopping Ads, and in-feed discovery, a 9:16 (vertical) ratio is essential to maximize screen real estate and avoid UI overlap [1.3.4].

**What are the file size limits for TikTok Shop?**
All images must be under 5MB. They must be in JPG, JPEG, or PNG format. If your file is larger, use a lossless compressor or lower the DPI to 72, which is the standard for web and mobile display.

**Can I use the same images for Amazon and TikTok Shop?**
You can use the same base product photo, but you should not use the same background. Amazon requires a pure white background (RGB 255, 255, 255) for the main image. TikTok audiences engage more with lifestyle-generated vertical hero shots that show the product in a relatable, real-world context.

**How many Shoppable Photos should I include in a post?**
TikTok allows up to 10 photos in a single Shoppable post. For maximum conversion, use a "Hero Shot" as the first image (9:16), followed by detail shots and a final "Size Chart" or "How to Use" graphic.

**Does TikTok Shop reject AI-generated images?**
No, TikTok Shop does not reject images simply because they are AI-generated. However, they will reject images that look "unnatural," have distorted product features (like blurred logos), or fail to meet the 600x600px minimum resolution requirement.
