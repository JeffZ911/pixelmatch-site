The new workflow prioritized three pillars: technical specs, visual integrity, and policy compliance.

### 1. Technical Precision
TikTok Shop has strict technical requirements for product imagery. To avoid automated rejection, the seller ensured every AI-generated lifestyle image met these verified specs:
*   **Resolution:** 1000 × 1000 px resolution is the gold standard, though the platform allows a 600 × 600 px minimum.
*   **File Size:** Every file was kept under the 5 MB limit to ensure fast loading on mobile devices.
*   **Format:** Standard JPEG or PNG formats were used to maintain color accuracy across different screen types.

### 2. Maintaining Product Integrity
The brand followed TikTok’s golden rule for AIGC: AI should only be used to enhance the *environment*, never the *product*. They used PixelMatch to generate lush, botanical backgrounds for their organic serums, but the bottle itself remained a real, unedited photograph. This prevented "Deceptive Content" flags, as the AI did not alter the product's color, size, or physical features.

### 3. The Disclosure Toggle
The most critical step in the new workflow happened during the upload process. When adding new images in the TikTok Shop Seller Center, the team explicitly enabled the “AI-generated content” toggle. This automatically applies the "AI-generated" badge to the image, signaling to both the algorithm and the consumer that the background is synthetic.

## Caveats and Honest Limitations

![Caveats and Honest Limitations](/img/tiktok-shop-ai-content-disclosure-badge-guide/inline-6.webp)


Audit your "Before/After" claims to ensure every visual proof point is a RAW, unedited photo to avoid permanent account suspension. While AI is a powerful tool for background generation, it is not a total replacement for photography in the beauty industry.

TikTok strictly prohibits using AI to fake "after" effects. For example, you cannot use AI to smooth a model's skin to imply your moisturizer works better than it does. You cannot use AI to lengthen eyelashes or brighten teeth in a way that misleads the consumer about the product's efficacy.

If your product relies on demonstrating physical results—such as a "7-day transformation"—you must use real, unedited photos for those specific claims. The AI [disclosure badge](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/policy/etsy-ai-model-disclosure-badge-guide) is mandatory for synthetic backgrounds, but it does not grant you a license to mislead. 

Additionally, while the AI [disclosure badge](https://pixelmatch.art/blog/policy/etsy-ai-model-disclosure-badge-guide) is a compliance requirement, its impact on consumer trust is still evolving.
